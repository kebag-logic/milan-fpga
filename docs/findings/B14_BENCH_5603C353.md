## Item 2: source switches (#645, #647)

**Method.** This is lane B8's case SW ([PR #646](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/pull/646)), repeated per cycle.
B8's three bindings are in place: the DUT's AAF talker to the peer's STREAM_INPUT 0, the peer's CRF talker to the DUT's STREAM_INPUT 1, and the peer's AAF talker to the DUT's STREAM_INPUT 0.
Each cycle sets the DUT's CLOCK_SOURCE to 2 (AAF), then 1 (CRF), then 0 (INTERNAL).
Each set is read back with GET_CLOCK_SOURCE.
The holds run 60 s after LOCKED on AAF, 45 s after LOCKED on CRF, and 15 s at INTERNAL.
Ten cycles give ten INTERNAL to AAF and ten AAF to CRF switches.

A console poll every 0.5 s reads the servo, the AAF meter, `SLIP_LB`, `SLIP_TDM` and `RENDER_STAT`.
GET_COUNTERS runs before each set, at LOCKED, 8 s after LOCKED, mid-hold and at the hold's end.
A tap capture runs from 3 s before each set to 17 s after it.

**The declared behaviour.** [MEDIA_CLOCK_FOLLOWING.md](../design/MEDIA_CLOCK_FOLLOWING.md#settle-recentre) fires the settle recentre 8 servo windows (4.096 s) after LOCKED.
From the set to that recentre the rings may move.
[The declared transient](../design/MEDIA_CLOCK_FOLLOWING.md#the-declared-transient) bounds an INTERNAL to AAF set at 3 frames at 5.92 ppm.
A stream-to-stream switch slips nothing.
After the recentre nothing moves either ring.

Here the settle boundary is the first poll that saw LOCKED, plus 4.096 s, plus 0.5 s.
On this lane `SLIP_LB` counts 2 per slipped frame: the peer's AAF stream feeds two loopback pairs.

**Result.**

- **INTERNAL to AAF, 10 of 10.** LOCKED 6.21 to 7.21 s after the set.
  `SLIP_LB` moved by 1, 2 or 3 frames before the boundary.
  The counts are 3, 2, 2, 3, 2, 1, 2, 2, 2 and 2 frames.
  This is inside the declared bound and reaches it in 2 switches.
  It never moved after the boundary in the 60 s holds.
  Three switches slipped once between LOCKED and the boundary: cycle 2 at 8.52 s, cycle 3 at 8.63 s and cycle 5 at 7.23 s.
- **AAF to CRF, 10 of 10.** LOCKED 2.71 to 3.40 s after the set. `SLIP_LB` never moved, before or after the boundary.
- **Both directions.** `SLIP_TDM` read 0/0 throughout.
  `RENDER_STAT`'s rail count never moved during a followed hold.
  The DUT's STREAM_INPUT 0 and 1 counted no MEDIA_UNLOCKED from LOCKED to the hold's end.
  CLOCK_DOMAIN counted LOCKED +1 and UNLOCKED +1 per switch.
  Each DUT talker counted MEDIA_RESET +1 per switch, and the peer's listener counted the same.
  No other counter moved except the traffic counters (FRAMES_TX, FRAMES_RX and TIMESTAMP_VALID).
  The servo ended every hold LOCKED, with its trim between -6.06 and -5.94 ppm.
- **Tap.** In all 20 captures, none of the four streams has a sequence gap.
  Each DUT talker toggles `mr` once, about 3.0 s into the capture, at the set.
  The peer's streams never toggle `mr`.
  Every AAF PDU has `tv=1` and `tu=0`.
  No presentation-time step departs from the stream's mode by more than 1 us.

**The settle recentre count.** The assignment asks that the declared settle recentre be counted once per switch.
No silicon register counts it.
[REGISTER_MAP.md](../reference/REGISTER_MAP.md#0x8dc-----render-setpoint-state) keeps the recentre counter as a verification tap.
`SLIP_LB` deliberately does not count the recentre's own step.
`RENDER_STAT`'s converged bit read 0 in 5 of the 10 AAF switches, 0.10 to 0.17 s after the set.
That is the #386 at-switch recentre, not the settle recentre.
No poll caught the bit near the settle point.
So "once per switch" is NOT RUN on this image: no silicon register or poll observes it.
What is observed is the recentre's declared effect: nothing moved after the boundary.

**Against #645 and #647.** #645 saw a one-frame slip 14.7 to 44.9 s after LOCKED, and lane B8 saw it 2.0 to 3.0 s after LOCKED, both on dev `bbf704ec`.
Here no switch slipped after its settle boundary, through holds of 60 s after LOCKED.
The followed receive path shows no undeclared discontinuity in 20 of 20 switches.

## Item 3: DUT-talker withdrawals (#608)

**Method.** This is lane B2's cycle ([608_75_WITHDRAWAL_AND_RESTART.md](608_75_WITHDRAWAL_AND_RESTART.md)), unchanged in grading.
The DUT's STREAM_OUTPUT 1 (CRF) feeds the peer's STREAM_INPUT 8.
The peer's CRF talker feeds the DUT's STREAM_INPUT 1, and the DUT's clock source is 1.
Each cycle captures 3 s, checks the stream flows, sends DISCONNECT_RX, holds 2 s, and sends CONNECT_RX.
It waits for the first valid PDU and captures 3 s more.
The tap keeps every frame except the VLAN-tagged AAF PDUs.
B2's analysis decodes every MSRPDU and grades the stop against the bridge's Listener `Lv` for the stream.

**Result: PASS, 100 of 100 (cycles 2 to 101).**

- The stream stopped within one PDU period (2 ms) of the bridge's `Lv` in every cycle.
  No valid PDU followed the `Lv` by more than one period.
  The last PDU preceded the `Lv` by 0.004 to 1.99 ms.
- The bridge's `Lv` crossed the tap 8.35 to 95.7 ms after the DISCONNECT_RX response, median 8.88 ms.
  It crossed the tap 57.5 to 95.7 ms after the response in cycles 26, 56 and 97; the stream ran until it, then stopped.
- The DUT's STREAM_OUTPUT 1 counted STREAM_START +1 and STREAM_STOP +1 in every cycle.
- The restart after CONNECT_RX took 10.4 to 125.9 ms, median 12.0 ms (#75: under 1 s).
- No MSRPDU was malformed. No tap record ran backwards.

**MSRP profile.** In 894 s of capture over the 100 cycles, the bridge sent 97 MRPDUs carrying a LeaveAll.
The DUT sent none.
That matters for #608's mechanism.
On `9e9954e9`, each of the 3 non-stops had the DUT's own LeaveAll within 0.1 s of the bridge's `Lv`.
On `13eda870`, lane B2's one LV case had the DUT's LeaveAll 1.390 ms before it.
B2 also saw a DUT LeaveAll less than 10 s after a received bridge LeaveAll in 67 of 112 captures.
Here no LeaveAll of either party preceded any `Lv` inside its window.
B2's classifier therefore reads the registrar at `Lv` as UNDETERMINED in all 100: neither a LeaveAll nor a re-declaration was seen before it.
The stop held in every cycle regardless.
Inside the 2 s hold:

- 86 cycles show only the bridge's Listener `Lv` and the DUT's Domain JoinIn and Talker Advertise JoinMt, besides the DUT's own Listener declaration for the peer's CRF stream;
- 12 cycles add a bridge LeaveAll of all four attribute types, after the `Lv`;
- 2 cycles (2 and 42) show the DUT withdrawing its Talker Advertise (`Lv`) 0.095 and 0.318 s after the bridge's `Lv`, which is 0.104 and 0.328 s after the DISCONNECT_RX response.

The pilot cycle 1, listed and not counted, shows the same withdrawal 0.039 s after the bridge's `Lv`.
No other cycle's capture holds a Talker Advertise `Lv` from the DUT.
Cycles 2 and 42 have the two slowest restarts, 105.7 and 125.9 ms; the next is 62.2 ms and the median 12.0 ms.
In the other 98 the DUT kept its Talker Advertise declared through the hold.
This is an observation for triage, not a failure of item 3.

Cycle 1 ran first and graded PASS, but its capture ended with rc 137 and no drop statistic.
The fix and its test are under [deviations](#method-deviations-and-authorities).
It is listed and not counted.

## Method, deviations and authorities

**Deviations from the lanes repeated.**

- **The peer's clock source, items 2 and 3.** As found, the peer follows the DUT's CRF.
  B8's and B2's methods make the DUT follow the peer.
  Kept as found, the two would form a clock loop.

- **Item 4.** B13 ran with the DUT's clock source 1 and the peer at INTERNAL.
