Unreachable in the page's cold-boot sequence; reachable with a writer restart that does not reset the fabric, with the second device-face initiator the page contemplates, or with any run-time RELOAD. Deleting the in-flight exception from the rule passes all 48 evidence cases. Rated MAJOR by the reviewer who executed it, MINOR by the other.
