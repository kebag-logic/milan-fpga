Found by the #645 round-2d timing gate ([STOP](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/issues/645#issuecomment-6041331463)).

## Finding

- The shipping image requires the Ethernet receive capture registers to be packed into the I/O cell (the #475 IOB-pack check).
- In #645 round 2d, synthesis remapped the synchronous reset of LiteEth's `milansoc_phy_source_valid_reg` (`eth0_rx_dv`) into a LUT2 in front of D, `rx_dv & ~rst`. A register with logic in front of D cannot be placed in the IOB.
- Place then raises 30-722 on `eth0_rx_dv` under all three directives, and the build stops before routing.
- The synthesized sources differ from #645 round 2c only by one unrelated term in `KL_chan_map_capture.sv`. Round 2c's inputs still re-synthesize correctly, and round 2d's inputs reproduce the failure, so the outcome is deterministic.
- **Any unrelated change can therefore tip IOB placement on this pin.** This is a latent fragility of the shipping image, not a defect of #645.

## Acceptance

1. Every GMII/RGMII RX capture register that the IOB-pack check requires is placed in its I/O cell regardless of the synthesis control-set heuristic. For example, the IOB input flops carry no synchronous reset (reset applied after the capture stage), or the reset is pinned by attribute or constraint. State the chosen mechanism and why it is robust.
2. The #475 IOB-pack check passes at dev and at #645 round 2d's tree.
3. The change does not alter receive behaviour. RX framing and reset semantics are verified by the existing Ethernet and datapath suites, and by a planted defect that restores the reset in front of D, which the IOB-pack check must refuse.
4. A full timing sweep passes, reporting WNS and WHS per directive, under the shipping recipe.
5. No change to the default build's function, the register map or the shipping image beyond the capture structure.
