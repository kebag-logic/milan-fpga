## Description

The declared CRF Media Clock Input (STREAM_INPUT index `N_STREAMS`) answered GET_COUNTERS
with an empty mask. `KL_crf_rx` kept all ten Milan Table 5.16 counters, but seven of them
and its dirty pulse were left open at the root. A Milan controller therefore rated the
entity IEEE 1722.1 only. This change closes the REGISTER_MAP closure criteria 1-4 at the
root. It makes no processor change and no pin change.

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| `hdl/milan/milan_datapath.sv` | The seven open `KL_crf_rx` tallies and `dirty_p_o` are connected. A new gather-face row (`ctr_crf_w`, `ctr_crf_block`) serves STREAM_INPUT `CRF_SNK_IDX_C`, only when the shape declares the CRF sink. It serves the ten at their Table 7-157 quadlets, full 32-bit, under `counters_valid = 0xF3F`. The Table 5.22 arbiter's STREAM_INPUT pending vector widens by the declared CRF sink (`PP_CTR_SIN_N_C`), so the pulse is delivered as {STREAM_INPUT, `N_STREAMS`}. |
| `hdl/ieee1722/crf/KL_crf_rx.sv` | Comments only. |
| `tb/verilator/milan_dp/sim_nxn.cpp` | `[CTRS-CRF]` (72 checks on each broad leg) and the timed leg's `[NOTIFY-CRF]` (10 checks). |
| `scripts/port_docs.budget` | Regenerated by the tool. |
| docs | REGISTER_MAP (the opening status, the gap closed, the frozen criteria quoted with their evidence), compliance matrix 5.4.2.25 -> `implemented`, audit B4, REGISTER_MAP_CLASSES, roadmap, FR_NFR, builder page, milan_dp README, CHANGELOG (unreleased). |

### Correction round 1 (`a51d5164`, `535701a0`)

This round changes no RTL.

**R269 F1 = R268 F1 (MAJOR; Conformance, Tests, Docs).** The signature arm seeds each root
tally wire by name. So it grades the gather mux and cannot see which `KL_crf_rx` output
drives a wire, and 7 of the 10 bindings could be exchanged with every leg green.

A new last arm of `[CTRS-CRF]` seeds no tally. In a fresh era, real PDUs at the root move
each of the ten through its own engine event:

- rejected PDUs;
- sequence gaps of one and of two or more;
- two locks;
- `mr` toggles and `tu` bits;
- timestamps behind the PHC and beyond the early limit;
- the engine's 100 ms silence timeout, for the unlock.

The ten end at pairwise-distinct counts (2, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 21), each checked
exactly at its quadlet. The Table 5.6 lock pair is also graded after the first lock (1/0)
and after the unlock (1/1).

**How the harness spends the silence.** It advances the engine's own timeout counter, through
the engine's hierarchy, to its last millisecond, and that millisecond runs for real.

- Run whole, the silence cost about 31 s on the nxn leg alone (6.2 s -> 37.6 s), on a suite
  with a hosted deadline.
- No tally is written. The unlock, its tally and every wire after it are the engine's.

**R268 F2 (MINOR; Tests, Docs).** After the one-second limit releases the withheld push,
`[NOTIFY-CRF]` now requires that the next two seconds without a change bring no further push,
to B and to A.

**R268 F3 (MINOR; Docs).** The REGISTER_MAP opening status no longer lists the CRF bank as a
remaining gap.

**R269 docs.** REGISTER_MAP, the harness comment and the milan_dp README now claim only what
the tests prove:

- the signature arm proves the mux;
- the event arm proves the bindings;
- each mutation turns at least one check red.

The frozen criteria are quoted beside their evidence.

**The mask interpretation**, which both reviewers confirmed: Milan keeps the IEEE Stream Input
offsets. Only Table 5.17 compacts, and only for Stream Outputs. TIMESTAMP_VALID and
TIMESTAMP_NOT_VALID stay unclaimed because `KL_crf_rx` keeps no tally for them. The AAF
inputs keep `0xFFF`.

**Area** (round 0, unchanged by this round): focused generic synthesis of `milan_datapath` goes
from 1,631,301 to 1,632,435 cells (+1,134, +0.07%).
