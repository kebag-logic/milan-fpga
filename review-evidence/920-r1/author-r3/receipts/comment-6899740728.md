[A10] **Ruling on the S12b rehearsal's findings 12 to 14 and its gPTP group (issuecomment-6899519003).** Maintainer-delegated, except where marked.

**1. Finding 13 (a burst of name changes is not saved in 10 s): fix before the flip. The owner is informed, with this as the default.**
- **The specification gives no time.** Milan v1.2 5.3.13 requires the names saved in non-volatile memory and restored after a power cycle. Neither Milan v1.2 5.4.2.11 nor IEEE 1722.1-2021 7.4.17 adds a time.
- **The 10 s is the scenario's.** It does not contradict a clause, so the scenario stands. The release passes it, and S12b requires in-house qualification all PASS, so this is a DUT defect.
- **The stated limit does not explain the failure.** The rehearsal image is 1x1. Its stated worst case with a save in flight is 6,070 ms plus 30 ms a record, starved. Alone it measured 2.0 s.
- **So the lane proves the cause first.** It replays the burst in the save harness and on the board, and reads the store's state before the cut. A failed attempt or a lost mark is fixed as that defect.
- **Target, harness:** at 1x1/1if and 8x8/1if, under the contract's held load, every acknowledged change is durable within 5 s of its response. That includes a burst marking every name, and a change marked during a save in flight.
- **Target, bench:** the persistence scenario passes at both shapes, three runs each. The time from the last response to durable is reported. STOP if it is over 8 s.
- **Allowed:**
  - a change marked during a running capture joins that capture;
  - the next save starts at the first idle step after the save in flight;
  - more than one step a centisecond, only in passes that took no input and have the step's time left, one step a pass.
- **Not allowed:**
  - a longer step, or a change to the age limit, the tiers or the pace;
  - any change to the two-slot sequence, the blank check, the verify or the format;
  - a shorter first-dirty window for a first change.
- **Budget:** every service-budget row within its bound at 1x1/1if and 8x8/1if, the save's windows re-measured, the load within 0.70 and 0.75. New groups carry planted defects caught by name. The power-cut tests stay green.
- **Still stated, not gated:** the starved worst case and the product part at its datasheet maximum stay above 10 s at 8x8. The design page says so; the append-only design (#823) closes them.
- **The owner may choose** to flip with the limit documented instead. The lane starts now on the default.

**2. Finding 14 (SET_STREAM_FORMAT refused with mappings out of range): the DUT is conformant. The harness changes.**
- **Milan v1.2 5.4.2.7 requires the refusal.** Before accepting, the entity checks that every channel referenced by an existing mapping still exists in the new format; if not, it refuses with BAD_ARGUMENTS.
- **IEEE 1722.1-2021 7.4.9.2 allows the same refusal**, with this very example.
- **Accepting would break Milan v1.2 5.3.10.1.** A mapped stream channel's index must be lower than the current format's channel count. The mappings stay as they were.
- **The "up to" entry is not the issue.** The 1-channel format is supported (Milan v1.2 5.3.8.1). The eight identity mappings on STREAM_PORT_INPUT 0 are what refuse it.
- **The release applies the same check.** It accepted on Oct 31 because the mappings then present survived the change.
- **Harness:** the scenario sets its own precondition. It removes the mappings the new format drops (Milan v1.2 5.4.2.28), sets the format, and restores them after.
- **Gating run, both ways:** with the mappings in place, BAD_ARGUMENTS and no state change. With them removed, SUCCESS, and the format survives the power cycle.
- **No DUT lane.** If a scenario as written expects SUCCESS with the mappings in place, the specification wins and the divergence is recorded.
