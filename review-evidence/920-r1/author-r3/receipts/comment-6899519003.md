**New findings:**
- **A. MAJOR, split-specific, not fixed by any composed PR: the split doesn't track the PHY link after boot.** The release polls the PHY over MDIO (`milan_baremetal.c:1048-1082`); the split's firmware publishes nothing, so `link_status` stays at its reset value 1. Consequences: LINK_UP/LINK_DOWN don't move across a flap, the link-edge counter notifications aren't sent, and a link toggle doesn't end the gPTP Pdelay cease. The release passed all three on 10-31. Fix: a PHY poll in the split platform that publishes `milan_mac_link_status`.
- **B. MAJOR, split-specific: a burst of name changes isn't saved within the plan's 10 s.** After the cold cycle, the last 5 of about 35 SET_NAMEs came back as defaults. The release passed on 10-31. Lead: the saved-state store's step rate (`ctrl_plat.c:273-277`).
- **C. Undetermined:** SET_STREAM_FORMAT to a 1-channel format covered by the input's "up to" entry answers BAD_ARGUMENTS. The processor's check also requires every mapped channel to survive the change. Needs a ruling: DUT or harness.
