   468	
   469	The binding walk owns the ACMP listener from reset until its last preload has been
   470	written and armed: commands, talker events, START/STOP requests and timer expiries wait
   471	at their producers until then, and `restore_done_o` marks that end
   472	([05 §5.1](05_acmp_engine.md#sec-05-boot-admission)). A read-only command that arrives
   473	during the walk is answered afterwards, from the restored image or, if the walk failed,
   474	from the vendor defaults, and it changes nothing here either way.
   475	
   476	**How a binding walk ends** (`KL_acmp_nvm_shadow`, processor issue #93). A walk is a
   477	transaction: a transport failure anywhere ends it with **every** sink not captured live at
   478	its vendor default, `restore_fail_o` and no preload, never with part of the image. It
   479	rejects the image, not the media: every saved record stays in the device as it was.
   480	
   481	| The record read… | Result | `restore_cause_o` |
   482	|---|---|---|
   483	| delivers a whole record that passes crc, layout_version, record_id and length | that sink's saved binding is stored and later preloaded | 0 |
   484	| delivers a whole record that fails one of those | **that record's** vendor default; the walk continues | 0 |
   485	| ends with nothing forwarded because the device answered with something that is not a record: a clean `done`, or an `err` the port names UNFRAMED ([02 §8](02_interfaces.md#fig-02-nvmwave)) | **that record's** vendor default; the walk continues | 0 |
   486	| ends after at least one forwarded byte and short of the record (torn) | the **whole walk** fails | 1 |
   487	| ends with nothing forwarded on an `err` the port names DEVICE (a device error, or a header read the device ended short) | the **whole walk** fails: a failing device is not an empty record | 2 |
   488	| waits `P-NVM-RS-TMO-CYC` consecutive clocks without progress (`T-NVM-RS-DEADLINE`): for the port idle before a read, or for a byte, `done` or `err` during it | the **whole walk** fails, and a read already issued is abandoned to the arbiter, which drains it ([02 §8.2](02_interfaces.md#82-two-record-managers-one-port)) | 3 |
   489	
   490	Progress is the awaited event itself, so a device that is slow but moving never trips the
   491	deadline, and the deadline bounds only the read phase: the preload phase is bounded by
   492	the listener's admission ([05 §5.1](05_acmp_engine.md#sec-05-boot-admission)). A failed
   493	walk still reaches its terminal, the listener is released and answers on the defaults,
   494	and an enable gated on `restore_done_o` follows. A walk that validated no record reports
   495	`restore_blank_o`, a failed one included. What the deadline does **not** do: it gives the
   496	port no deadline of its own and releases nothing on time. A device that ends the
   497	abandoned read late ends the drain and the port serves the next operation; a device that
   498	never ends it leaves the port quarantined until reset.
   499	
   500	**A failed walk keeps the saved records** (processor issue #92). After the atomic reject
   501	the listener runs on its defaults, and it writes its record back for every command it
   502	serves, a read-only `GET_RX_STATE` included. The shadow commits only a record that differs
   503	from what it holds, and **two unbound records never differ**, whatever their other fields:
   504	an unbound record carries no binding (Milan v1.2 5.3.8.3 clears the binding parameters on
   505	unbind), and no walk preloads one. So a command that leaves a sink unbound writes nothing
   506	after a failed walk, whether it is a `GET_RX_STATE` held from the boot window, a later
   507	poll, or an `UNBIND` of a sink the failed walk left unbound, and the next walk on a
   508	healthy device restores every saved binding. A `BIND` does bind the sink: it replaces
   509	that sink's record, as it would after any walk. Graded for all three causes in
   510	[`tb/acmp_nvm`](../../tb/acmp_nvm/README.md) (N8) and at the top in
   511	[`tb/pp_top`](../../tb/pp_top/README.md) (BW3).
