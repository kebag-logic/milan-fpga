**Item 4 (CRF), evidence only, nothing changed.** The Equation (14) round-up does not cover the CRF path's own latency.
- 125,000 ns is exactly 6 periods (125,000 x 3 / 62,500) and 2 ms is exactly 96, so `KL_mtt_round`'s ceiling adds 0 ns at both offsets.
- `KL_crf_tx` stamps the 96th event's instant plus that offset (`KL_crf_tx.sv:511`). It hands the PDU over B + 3 to 10 cycles later: 21,024 to 21,164 ns at 1x1 and 5,368 to 5,508 ns at 8x8. Add up to one AAF PDU in flight at `crf_dp_mux` (600 ns) and any control frame at `adp_tx_mux`.
- So the CRF timestamp sits TTmax - 21 us after the 1x1 hand-over. IEEE 1722-2016 10.7 asks for at least TTmax.
- The term Equation (14) has for this is TC, "the amount of time that samples spend accumulating in the Talker's transmit buffer". The RTL adds no TC. Since #770 back-dated the CRF stamp by B, CRF is short by B plus its pipeline at every whole-period offset, the factory 2 ms included. That matches the roughly 20 us CRF shift on the bench.

**A constraint on any fix.** Both outputs stamp instants of one frame grid, each plus its own offset. So a constant added to the AAF stamp keeps #770's 4.3.5 relation and the 10.8 band against the CRF timing points only if it is a whole number of periods, within +/-5 % of Ps.
- 7 Ps (145,833.3 ns) covers the settled-lock maxima of both shapes, leaving 2.1 us at 1x1 for MAC-side interference.
- 8 Ps (166,666.7 ns, stamped as 166,667 ns, 0.33 ns over) covers every state, including 148.8 us before a lock at 1x1, plus a maximum-size frame ahead at the MAC.
- Equation (1)'s lower bound (TT > PT - TTmax - TUmax, with TUmax 125 us) holds for any constant up to the earliest hand-over plus 125 us: 252.99 us at 1x1 and 237.27 us at 8x8.

**Decision needed:**
- (a) An owner exception to Milan v1.2 5.3.7.6's "between 0 and 125 microseconds": stamp M + 8 Ps + the offset as set at both shapes. That is one constant, whole periods, folded into the existing stamp adder or the banked instant. GET_STREAM_INFO reads the offset as set. End-to-end latency = offset + 166,667 ns.
- (b) The same stamp, read as 5.3.7.6 states its two parts. The Ingress Time Reference Plane crossing is estimated as M + 6 Ps: the end of the PDU's six-frame span (at 1x1, the earliest instant its data exists; at 8x8 the kept pairs close a quarter into the sixth frame, 0.75 Ps earlier). The fixed delay of 2 Ps (41.7 us) then compensates that estimate's uncertainty, inside 125 us at both shapes. The worst hand-over is 23.8 us after the estimate at 1x1 and 22.7 us at 8x8.
- For CRF, the same choice decides whether it adds a whole-period TC: 2 Ps at 1x1 (its path is 21.0 to 21.8 us, over one period) and 1 Ps at 8x8.

Recommendation: (b), with CRF's TC as whole periods. It gives the same RTL as (a), needs no exception to 5.3.7.6's bound, and keeps the clause's estimate and its fixed delay apart. Shrinking the pipeline is not an option at 1x1 (A + B > 125 us). A base other than the frame instant would undo #770's 4.3.5 alignment.
