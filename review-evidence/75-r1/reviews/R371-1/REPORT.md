### F2 - MINOR - Robustness, Tests, Docs - page lines 87-98 and 121-126; packet `tools/action.py` / `tools/analyze.py` - For intervals under one PDU period the page cannot show the stream had stopped; the stop check is recorded but not asserted or published
- Authority/evidence: the measured interval ends at the first valid PDU at or after the response (`analyze.py`: `r['ns']>=ack['ns']`). The CRF streams advance once per 2 ms: next minus first timestamp is 2,000,009 ns and 2,000,022 ns in the public cycles. An interval under 2 ms therefore cannot tell "resumed at the response" apart from "already flowing at the response". Three talker cycles fall there: 13 (0.000364 s), 24 (0.000297 s, the published minimum) and 75 (0.001864 s). The acquisition computes `frames_last_half_second_disconnected`, but `action.py` records it without asserting it. `analyze.py` does not check it. It is published only for cycles 1-5, and it does not cover the connect-command-to-response window. The published size index adds a signal (`receipts/capture_size_vs_latency.txt`). All three sub-period cycles are among the six largest talker captures, ranks 2, 3 and 6 of 100, a placement with chance probability about 1.2e-4. Their excess is +52 kB and +109 kB over the median, about 1 s and 2 s of one CRF stream. Other explanations are possible, such as a longer capture tail while readbacks run inside the capture. The bridge withdrew Listener in every talker cycle (Lv = 100), which argues against the stream never stopping, but the page does not establish it. In all 100 listener cycles and 97 talker cycles the interval exceeds a PDU period, so the stream was absent at the response and the first valid PDU after it is the first of the restart. Skipping invalid PDUs of the target stream only lengthens the interval, which is conservative.
- Impact: the published minimum and the talker lower tail may not be restart times. The validation chain has a check that could catch a missing stop, but it cannot fail a cycle. Acceptance is unaffected either way, since earlier resumption only helps criterion 1.
- Required outcome: from the retained captures, the page or its ledger states for every numbered cycle that the target stream was absent from the disconnect settling to the connect response, or equivalently the valid target-PDU count in that window. Cycles 13, 24 and 75 are explained. Either the stream resumed before the response (report it as resumed at or before the response, censored at zero) or it did not stop (report it separately and recount toward the 100-cycle requirement). The size excess of the six largest talker captures is explained.
