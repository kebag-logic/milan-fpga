== docs/design/SAVED_STATE_FASTCONNECT.md:661-676
   661	**The erased-record rule (round 5, 2026-09-06).** A record position may hold,
   662	instead of a frame, the record's whole span (8 header bytes plus its
   663	payload_length) filled with `0xFF`. That record has never been written since
   664	the media was blank: the backend erases a record's span to `0xFF` and writes
   665	it in place, the firmware wraps the record area verbatim, and a record the
   666	processor has not yet committed therefore has no frame to carry. An erased
   667	record counts as PRESENT for rule 12, applies nothing, and the processor
   668	restores the vendor default for it through the same blank arm it takes on
   669	blank media. The rule is deliberately narrow: an erased span is accepted only
   670	at the position of the next required record in ascending id order and only
   671	for that record's exact span, so a header of `0xFF` over a payload that is not
   672	erased is a torn or foreign span and is refused (rule 8), and an image that is
   673	one record SHORT is still refused (rules 10 and 12), because absence stayed an
   674	omission. Without this rule a board that has never been bound could never
   675	carry an accepted image at all, and the very first controller SET would have
   676	produced a container rule 12 refuses.
