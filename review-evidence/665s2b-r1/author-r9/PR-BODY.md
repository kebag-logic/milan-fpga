## Known limitations / out of scope

- **The firmware's own host suites** still hold their paths to 10 ms (`aecp_latency_policy.hpp`, `srp_latency_policy.hpp`, `test_maap_mbx.cpp`). That is tighter than the approved bounds, so they stay valid. Whether they move to min(20 ms, the ceiling) is a separate decision; their planted "relaxed budget" defects would move with them.
- **The merge with `dev-soft-core`:** it now carries S1's PR #795, and a trial merge conflicts in eleven files, among them FR_NFR.md and the harness's `split_soc.py` and `build.py`. S1 adopts AECP IN_PROGRESS for a face-state change, and its FR_NFR row says that cadence's 12 ms ceiling "is still above T_svc". Under the approved rule that path's bound is 12 ms, and the merge must say so.
- **The controls are the cacheless core's.** Behind the split CPU's own caches the planted spin takes 17.3 ms and fits the budget.
- **Not in this round:** the block-RAM ledger (124 against 121.5), and the drafts' own repository reviews (lwSRP's and the TSN stack's). Each needs its repository's review, then a pin move.
- **The measurement uses labelled variants:** round 6's profiling variant (which sets the CPU clock), and the drafts staged in scratch clones.
