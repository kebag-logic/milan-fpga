**(4) The fan-out.** I traced `aecp.c:609-683` against `mbx_tx_begin`, which commits the record before its own first (`mbx.c:536-548`):
- **REFUSED mid-walk.** The frame before it is committed and `k` stays at the refused recipient, whose sequence is not spent. `fan_end(whole=false)` commits nothing and keeps nothing. The walk resumes at that recipient.
