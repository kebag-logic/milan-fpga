## Description

The maintainer decisions of 2026-11-05 on #840, items 1 to 4.

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| Configs | AX7101 station MAC `02:4b:4c:00:00:01`, Arty `02:4b:4c:00:00:02`. entity_id and clockIdentity stay MAC-derived: `024B4CFFFE000001` and `024B4CFFFE000002`. |
| Builder | `identity_warnings()` warns on stderr, never refuses, when the station MAC has the U/L bit set. Gate 28b (`test_station_mac_ul_bit_warns`) pins every config's decided identity and the warning's channel and exit status. |
| Firmware | `phy_advertise_gigabit_only()`: register 9 with 1000BASE-T full duplex only (MASTER-SLAVE bits kept), register 4 without 10/100 or 100BASE-T4, then one auto-negotiation restart. It writes only a PHY reporting 1000BASE-T full duplex ability (bits 1.8 and 15.13). |
| Firmware test | The pin-level Clause 22 peer records writes and restarts. The host driver grades each property by name, the retry after an unacknowledged read, and two PHYs without the ability. Twelve planted advertisement mutants each fail their named assertion, `advertise-100base-tx` by `no_100base_tx_advertised`. |
| Service budget | The scripted PHY accepts Clause 22 writes. The poll charge counts the init's four reads and three writes (sixteen transactions). `oracle.json` is regenerated by `gen_oracle.py` from fresh simulations of both shapes. |
| Capture | The harness addresses its controller traffic to the config's station MAC and MAC-derived entity_id (`probe_config.hpp`). `measurements.json` is re-measured: six arms at the head that carries the firmware. |
| Fixtures | The gptp leg and the AX 1x1 gPTP leg carry the shipping image's identity (MAC CSRs, entity_id, thisClock, the gPTP ROM's `--mac`). Also the command table entry for that Makefile line, and the bench tools' board defaults. |
| Docs | New [stricter readings page](docs/reference/STRICTER_READINGS.md), linked from `docs/README.md` and registered with the style gate. Also the firmware guide's PHY section, the builder parameter docs and the service-budget README. |

**Material assumption: the 1000BASE-T ability gate.** The issue says "the MAC is 1 Gb/s only". That holds for the AX7101's GMII build. The retired Arty recipe (`phy: mii-100`) pairs a 10/100 PHY with a MAC that runs 10 and 100 Mb/s (`sw/litex/milan_soc.py`, `_BOARD_PHY_MODEL` and `_PHY_MODEL_MIN_MBPS`). The Arty platform routes MDIO, so its firmware compiles the same PHY init. Unconditionally, the init would strip that PHY's only abilities, and an Arty image would never link. The init therefore writes only a PHY whose bits 1.8 (IEEE 802.3-2022 22.2.4.2.16) and 15.13 (22.2.4.4.3) report 1000BASE-T full duplex ability. Any other PHY keeps its own advertisement, as at the base. On the AX7101 the behaviour is exactly the issue's.

**Identity sources.** The station identity is produced in this repository only: `platform.mac_address`, `derive_entity_id()` and the builder's `--mac` to the gPTP microcode generator. A configs-only sweep (four shards) failed in `fw_service_budget` alone ("oracle image changed"). So harness-chosen identities, such as `sw/firmware/ctrl/test/test_aecp.cpp:46-47` and the avtp and crf stream ids, assert no config identity and are left as they are. Bench findings pages keep the identity they measured.

**certification_version** stays `0.0.0.0` (item 3). It is a GET_MILAN_INFO response field (Milan v1.2 5.4.4.1, Figure 5.4), not a descriptor field. Tests asserting it already exist: check M2 of the protocol processor's `tb/pp_top` harness (the real microprogram), and `tests/features/aecp_response_contract.feature:150-154` (the model). So no test is added.
