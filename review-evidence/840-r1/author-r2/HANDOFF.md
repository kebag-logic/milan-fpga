## F3: what was found and changed

- The reviewers saw T grow 8,793 -> 16,963 and P 65,294 -> 247,411 cycles and read the writes as
  the slow transactions. A per-transaction probe (scratch copy of `phy.hpp`, receipts/phy-cost-probe/)
  shows every transaction, reads included, at 16,855-16,957 cycles at dd764ed7.
- Cause: `phy_mdio_write()` gave `phy_mdio_bit()` a second caller and GCC stopped inlining it. On
  the cacheless core each bit became a call and return: 16 instructions and three taken branches
  where the base had 6 and one (disassembly receipts).
- Fix (c06150b4): `static inline __attribute__((always_inline))`. `phy_mdio_read()` compiles to
  the base's 65 instructions, identical. Measured at M: reads 8,649-8,795 cycles, writes up to
  9,534, discovery poll 131,480 (1x1) and 131,864 (8x8), steady poll 46,470 (was 88,839).
- Charge and reserve (all six plans per shape, per-shape maxima, as the page builds them):
  8x8 T 0.12457 ms, P 1.31864 ms, C 3.31176 ms, reserve 3.19136 ms (base 4.46039);
  1x1 T 0.12451 ms, P 1.31480 ms, C 3.30696 ms, reserve 41.82117 ms (base 43.81594).
- Largest per-run PHY publication bound: 8x8 246.80864 ms (`milan_nvm`, uart-paced, own charge
  3.31176 ms); 1x1 207.70251 ms (`mem_read`, queued-builtins).
- "Before" evidence: the four enforced 8x8 plans on dd764ed7's firmware (a clean clone of
  dd764ed7, scratch before/; receipts/before-dd764ed7/). All four ended rc 0 with no service finding.
  Their largest PHY publication bound was 248.68507 ms (`milan_nvm`, queued-input, own charge
  5.18819 ms), 1.31493 ms under 250; the same plan at M grades 246.34096 ms. So no enforced plan
  failed at dd764ed7, and the assignment's "if any plan fails, lower the cost" condition did not
  arise. The cost was lowered anyway because the measured cause is this lane's own doubling of
  every PHY transaction, read or write, and the inlining removes it with the base's own code. The
  bound and the trigger are unchanged.
