## Behaviour (IEEE 802.1AS-2011 with Cor1 and Cor2; Milan v1.2)

- portEnabled (10.2.4.11) is MAC_Operational and the port administratively enabled. The parent drives its effective link, eff_link.
- Milan v1.2 4.2.6.2.5 ends the multiple-responder cease when the link state or portEnabled toggles, or after 5 minutes.
- Figure 11-8 (as corrected by Cor1 and Cor2): `!portEnabled` enters NOT_ENABLED. It is left only through INITIAL_SEND_PDELAY_REQ, which sends a Pdelay_Req at once and sets `lostResponses = 0` and `asCapable = FALSE`.
- **Falling edge (NOT_ENABLED).**
  - The engine queues a timer-slot-6 event. The slot dispatch, which ended there before, now runs the new NOTEN leg.
  - NOTEN clears `S_CEASE`, `S_CEASECNT`, `S_MULTI`, `S_IVMULTI` and `S_RSP1` and sets `S_PDGOT`. Then it takes PDPAIR's bad-verdict tail: `S_PDOK = 0`, asCapable cleared, COMMIT.
  - asCapable falls with a commit one handler after the edge (the engine suite bounds it at 2 ms), not at the fifth lost response 4 s later.
  - The engine's existing asCapable-fall hook then drops an elected peer (the #82 DISABLED path). A master keeps its role and sync-ok but sends nothing.
- **While the port is disabled** (10.2.4.11; a DisabledPort, 10.3, transmits neither Announce nor Sync):
  - the credit word reads 0, so the Pdelay_Req, Sync and Announce beats skip through the existing credit gate;
  - every SEND is dropped, so a frame built across the fall, or a reply to a frame heard while disabled, does not leave;
  - both egress claims read empty: a stamp the down link never returns cannot block a later beat, and a late stamp builds no Follow_Up;
  - received frames still dispatch. Nothing they would send leaves, and asCapable gates Announce and Sync processing as before.
- **Rising edge (INITIAL_SEND_PDELAY_REQ).** NOTEN runs again, then a slot-0 beat queued right behind it. One Pdelay_Req goes out at once and re-arms the 1 s cadence from itself. `S_PDGOT` set means that beat counts no lost response (`lostResponses = 0`). asCapable stays FALSE until two good exchanges (Milan v1.2 4.2.6.2.4).
- **First enable since reset.** It raises no event; the boot cadence serves it, as before. Boot is therefore unchanged whenever the port is up at reset release: every repository suite, and the parent's simulations, which drive the PHY link high from reset while the link guard reports alive until its first toggle. On a board whose link comes up after reset, nothing is sent until it does, and the first request goes out at the next beat after it.
- **Countdown exit unchanged.** The beat that ceases sends nothing, the countdown runs, its last beat resumes and the next sends. The first request after the cease follows the last before it by the countdown plus two beats: 302 s at 5 minutes (the issue measured 302.0 s), 5 s in the 3 s test images.
- **ROM.** The cease entry now ends in NOTEN's tail (the interval clears, then PDPAIR's bad verdict). That tail writes the same cells and commits the same flags, and the sharing saves 8 words, which pay for the 8-word NOTEN leg. A 3,000-configuration packing sweep against base finds the same used count in every configuration and no packing failure (`receipts/rom/`).
- **Not implemented, recorded.** Cor2 also sets `neighborRateRatio = 1.0` and `pdelayReqSequenceId = random()` in INITIAL_SEND_PDELAY_REQ. The engine keeps its rate window and its sequence counter across the toggle. After an outage longer than 2^32 ns the window is stale and skipped, as before. A shorter one may give the first exchange a ratio spanning two peers; the ladder still needs two good exchanges, which stay inside 4.2.6.2.4's 2 to 5.
- **Corner, recorded.** A warm reset taken during a cease with the port low across the reset: the first enable after it raises no event, so that cease runs on to its countdown. A cold boot clears the cease anyway, and a link toggle in the parent does not reset the engine.
