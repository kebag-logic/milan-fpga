Interpreted scope: the defect comes first. `KL_reboot_ctrl` gains an egress phase between the saved-state drain and the PHY hold. When the phase starts, the datapath's MAC port finishes the frame in progress and starts no new one. The PHY hold and the IPROG wait then wait until every frame the MAC has taken has completed at the PHY interface, plus one quiet millisecond, or until a bound. The bound is a named parameter derived from one maximum frame plus every MAC transmit buffer, framing included, at the slowest line rate. The SoC derives it from the constants its FIFOs are built with; it is not a copied figure. The 13,502 ms bound is restated.
Signal used: the datapath counts the frames it hands to the MAC at its own `m_axis_mac_tx` port. The MAC returns one flip of a new reset-less toggle per frame, on the last octet of each frame at LiteEth's `phy.sink`: the GMII/MII transmit stream one register stage before the pads, the seam `KL_gptp_gmii_launch` already observes. The toggle is synchronised like the link guard's three toggles. The count is cleared while the link guard holds the whole MAC transmit side in reset. Everything is parent code (`sw/litex/milan_soc.py`, `hdl/milan`); no submodule changes. The STOP for a missing or submodule-bound egress signal is therefore not met.
Tests: a datapath-level check in `tb/verilator/pp_shadow` section RB, at the wire-side output of a MAC model with a store-and-forward FIFO and a serialiser, not at the datapath ports. On an advertising REBOOT, the SUCCESS response, the notification and ENTITY_DEPARTING leave whole and in order before `phy_hold_o`. On a non-advertising REBOOT there is no DEPARTING, and the PHY still waits for the last started frame. A dead wire still releases the hold at the bound. Planted mutants, each required to fail: the PHY hold on the request alone (today's behaviour), a drain that ignores the MAC FIFO, a drain with no bound, and an early request; unit-level arms in `tb/verilator/reboot_ctrl`.
