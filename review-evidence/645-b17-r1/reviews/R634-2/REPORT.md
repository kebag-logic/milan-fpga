## Findings

### R634-2-F1 MINOR: the page's burst definition does not reproduce two printed burst ranges

- **Lenses:** Conformance, Docs.
- **Where:** `:4890` ("Bursts of PDUs over 20 us late per cycle, 3 to 12 consecutive PDUs each"). It governs
  `:4891` (11 to 24), `:4895` (10 to 23) and `:4902`, and the PR body's round 2 bullet ("10 to 23 bursts",
  "11 to 24 to the capture's end").
- **Evidence:** The summary tool (`tools/summ_b17.py`, docstring and `bursts()`) groups PDUs over 20 us late
  "while consecutive PDUs are less than 1 ms apart". So a burst can span PDUs at or under 20 us; the largest
  gap inside a burst is 504 us, about four PDU periods.
  - Two bursts are not runs of consecutive PDUs: c02 at 6.17852 s (9 PDUs, 504 us gap) and c07 at 23.44078 s
    (10 PDUs, 257 us gap).
  - Under the page's stated definition (consecutive at the 125 us PDU period), bursts per cycle to the
    capture's end are 12 to 24, not 11 to 24, and bursts after the slip to the capture's end are 11 to 23, not
    10 to 23. The to-the-last-poll ranges, the peaks, 3 to 12 PDUs and 9 of 10 are unchanged.
  - The 1 ms rule is not stated anywhere in the B17 section.
  - Receipt: `receipts/burst-definition-probe.json`, script `scripts/burst_definition_probe.py`.
- **Impact:** Round 2 exists to make every printed figure reproducible from the recorded data, under the window
  and basis the page states. A reader who applies the page's definition to the published `late_pdus` gets two
  different ranges. The slip times, classes and verdict do not change.
- **Required outcome:** The page's burst definition and the printed burst figures agree, in either direction:
  - state the grouping actually used (PDUs over 20 us late, grouped while less than 1 ms apart, 3 to 12 such
    PDUs each) and drop "consecutive"; or
  - regenerate the burst figures under a consecutive-PDU rule.

  The PR body bullet follows the page.
- **Verification:** `scripts/burst_definition_probe.py` (or the lane's tool) reproduces every printed burst
  range under the definition the page states.

  first poll after the bind already read prefill 0, and in the other five the poll before it started earlier
