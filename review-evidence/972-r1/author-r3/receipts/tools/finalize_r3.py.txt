| `linkreset` and `linkreset-plants` | `77a74b2f` | 4 of 4 arms PASS; 2 of 2 plants caught |
| Split boot simulation, both shapes and `--self-test` | `77a74b2f` | 1x1 and 8x8 PASS: ready 27.940 / 67.214 ms; the arm's link down at 2.637 / 4.437 ms, before the first frame at 27.096 / 66.163 ms; 6 of 6 native controls, 7 of 7 plants, 8 of 8 placement controls |

| Routes, three directives at each shape (the gating recipe, `vivado-slot.sh`) | `93d9eaaf` | all six meet #818 at every corner. 1x1: ExtraPostPlacementOpt +0.331 / +0.031 ns (best), AltSpreadLogic_high +0.130 / +0.036, ExtraTimingOpt +0.133 / +0.036. 8x8: ExtraPostPlacementOpt +0.176 / +0.036 (best), AltSpreadLogic_high +0.150 / +0.036, ExtraTimingOpt +0.059 / +0.036 (worst-corner WNS / WHS). for name in ('PR-BODY.md', 'REVIEW-READY.md'):
    p = R / name
    t = p.read_text()
    t = t.replace('GATES_TABLE', table).replace('MILAN_HEAD_FULL', head)
    t = t.replace('control campaign CAMPAIGN_COUNT', 'control campaign 837 of 837 caught')
    t = re.sub(r'^DRAFT:.*\n\n', '', t)
    t = re.sub(r'^WIP: .*$', f'GREEN. Every gate passes (the table below), at local head `{head}`.', t, flags=re.M)

    assert not left, (name, left)
    p.write_text(t)
print('filled', head)
