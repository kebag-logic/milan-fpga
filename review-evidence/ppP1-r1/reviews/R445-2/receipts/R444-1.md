### RTL

Name stage: `KL_aecp_nvm_writer.sv` restore and service, `KL_aecp_engine.sv` taps, `KL_aecp_desc_store.sv` region 0xA.

- **Restore.**
  - A name READ fills the shared 8×64 LUT-RAM buffer lane by lane (`rd_lane_w`, `{rval_r[55:0], m_rdata_i}` at payload byte j%8 = 7, guarded `rpj_w < 64`).
  - The write-back (`W_NAPPLY`) happens only after `frame_ok_w` (magic, layout, record id, payload_length = 64, crc16) and the region-0xA ordinal rule (`W_NNAME`).
  - A torn read aborts and a blank record keeps the image's name. An abort during the write-back rolls back, and the descriptor store's walk reloads the names.
- **Service.**
  - `S_NLATCH` holds each lane read until it is answered, while the writer owns the bus, so no µCPU write can interleave.
  - `nchg_i = store_name_wr_w && !d3_bus_w`, so the writer's own restore pulses neither the change nor `aecp_name_wr_o`.
  - The ordinal is `st_addr_w[15:6]`, combinational with the store's `take_wr_w`.
  - Restore and service never share the buffer (`done_r` selects the ports).
- **Bound.** `N_NAME_P` 1..128 is refused at elaboration beyond the 0x80-0xFF block. The record id is `0x80 + ordinal`.
- **ROMs.** Regenerated at the base, both merge parents and the head, they are byte-identical (`receipts/roms_sha256.txt`): `ucode.hex`, `ltn_rom.hex`, and the `milan_min` and `example_milan_8` images.
