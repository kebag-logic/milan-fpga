== Milan v1.2 consolidated (Final Approved 20231130), Annex B.1.1 and 7.4, pdftotext -layout
B.1.1 tu (timestamp uncertain) field

In case of a change of grandmaster, the tu bit shall be set to 1 for the duration of 0.25 seconds.

7.4 Media Clock Source Quality

In accordance to [AES11], oscillators used as source in a media clock domain shall produce a clock whose
frequency tolerance is better than +/-50PPM.
== IEEE 802.1AS-2020 Annex B.1.1
 B.1 LocalClock requirements

 B.1.1 Frequency accuracy

 The fractional frequency offset of the LocalClock relative to the TAI frequency (see ISO 80000-3:2006 and
 Annex C) shall be within ± 100 ppm.
