### 2. R793-4 F6 and R794-4 S9: the held-probe conditions

- **PORTING:110** now reads: "at most one per other sink while no sink's probing restarts (an ADP re-advertisement that does not raise the available index, a new discovery after its talker departs or its advertisement times out, or `acmp_tk_unregistered`) and the held probe has waited less than TMR_NO_RESP (200 ms); past that, another sink's TMR_NO_RESP retry can also be owed before it."
  - PORTING:114 (gated port) uses the same "probing restarts".
  - The PR body says the same in Round 4 item 3 and Round 5 item 3.
  - The five new phrases are pinned (`check_port_contracts.py:303-307`).
- **The three restart paths match the code.**
  - Re-advertisement: `disc_available` with `index <= disc_available_index` calls `tk_departed`, which stops a probing sink, and then `tk_discovered`, which arms TMR_DELAY (`src/acmp.c:672-678`).
  - Departure or timeout, then a new discovery: `disc_departing` and the ADP deadline call `tk_departed`. A later `disc_available` calls `tk_discovered` (`:659-666`, `:686-693`, `:1187-1189`).
  - `acmp_tk_unregistered` calls `reprobe` from SETTLED_RSV_OK (`:1227-1238`).
  - The other re-probe paths need no listing:
    - TMR_NO_TK's `reprobe` (`:823`) needs 10 s of SETTLED_NO_RSV, so it cannot give a sink a second probe inside a 200 ms wait.
    - A BIND_RX's own PROBE_TX is counted among the frames owed by kept commands.
- **The 200 ms condition is right, and it is needed.**
  - Another sink's first probe in the wait leaves at or after the held probe falls due, and its TMR_NO_RESP runs from when it leaves (`:709-713`, `:751-760`). So its retry cannot be owed before a held probe that has waited less than 200 ms.
  - My unchanged `probe_r4c.cpp` `sustained` case prints the same line as in round 4 (`receipts/probes/r4c-head.txt`): sink 2 leaves in passes 11 and 250, and sink 3 in pass 251. The new sentence describes exactly this.
- **"until the probe is owed or cancelled" (PORTING:94)** holds. In my new probe `probe_r5.cpp`, a held TMR_NO_RESP retry keeps its sink in PRB_W_RESP for 5600 passes. It leaves only when the commands stop (`receipts/probes/r5-head.txt`).
- **What can be owed past 200 ms: the sentence is true, but it names only the retry (RES8).**
  - `probe_r5b.cpp` gives the port no command in two passes, so the two lowest held retries (sinks 1 and 2) take the freed slots and sink 3 stays held.
  - Commands then resume on every pass. Sink 2 goes PRB_W_RESP2 → PRB_W_RETRY → (TMR_RETRY 4 s) → PRB_W_DELAY.
  - Its next TMR_DELAY probe is also owed before sink 3's held retry: three probes of sink 2 in one wait (passes 11, 310 and 6409, then sink 3 in pass 6410), with no restart (`receipts/probes/r5b-head.txt`).
  - Without the gap, sink 2 owes exactly two (`r5-head.txt`), because a held retry does not advance its sink.
  - This is the normal 5.5.3.5.23/.30/.10 cycle, not a restart. No figure and no Milan deadline depend on it: the held probe's own TMR_NO_RESP runs from when it leaves. So it is wording only (RES8).
