[A847]

Seed the ADP delay on each transition out of DOWN from the MAC address, the clock, the entity ID and the interface

Closes #39. Relates to kebag-logic/milan-fpga#970.

## What

The ADP core seeded its delay generator once, at `adp_set_enable`, with `rng ^= seed()`, and drew from the top 16 bits
of one xorshift32 step. Now `leave_down` takes one sample from `seed()` on each transition out of DOWN: the startup
draw with the link up (Milan v1.2 5.6.3.5.2) and each LINK_UP from DOWN (5.6.3.5.3). It seeds with the least
significant 32 bits of the sum of the MAC address and the sample: the plain sum, modulo 2^32. That sum is mixed with the earlier
state, then with both words of the entity ID and the interface, by two rounds of a multiply-free bijection.

- A duplicate LINK_UP, a LINK_UP while disabled, an enable with the link down, and every other draw (DISCOVER,
  TMR_ADVERTISE, GM_CHANGE) take no sample.
- The generator never draws from zero. A seed that mixes to zero becomes 1 (A22, plant `seed-left-at-zero`), and no
  draw is reachable before the first transition out of DOWN: every other call of `enter_delay` requires WAITING.
- The draw ranges, `draw_ms` and xorshift32 (13, 17, 5) are unchanged.
- `adp_init` no longer seeds: the first transition out of DOWN always does.
- The seeding is `leave_down`'s own body, so the compiler does not fold it into `adp_link_change`.

## Why

- IEEE 1722.1-2021 6.2.4.2.2 asks for:
  - "a uniform distribution across the range";
  - an algorithm "chosen so that different hosts do not generate the same sequence of numbers";
  - "a maximum length sequence of no less than 2^32 - 1";
  - a generator "seeded using the least significant (most rapidly varying) octets of the sum of the requestor's IEEE
    802 MAC address and the local real time clock".
- The least significant 32 bits of that sum are the plain sum modulo 2^32. The carry out of bit 31 is not folded back.
- The core used no MAC address and no sum. One xorshift step does not carry a seed's low bits into the 16 bits a draw
  uses. In the parent's firmware, every boot of one entity drew its first delay between 640 and 890 ms, and sixteen
  neighbouring entity IDs drew within 5 ms of each other.
- Two interfaces seeded together drew alike, and a LINK_UP after the enable drew from the enable's state, so the time of
  the link never reached the seed. milan-fpga#970's ruling of 2026-11-25 requires a fresh sample mixed with the entity ID
  and the interface at each qualified LINK_UP and at the startup draw.

## Integrators

The `seed` callback is now called once per transition out of DOWN, after `link_up`. It returns the least significant 32
bits of the local clock, at its finest resolution, as it is asked. `docs/PORTING.md` states that contract, and
`examples/adp_port.c` returns its clock.
