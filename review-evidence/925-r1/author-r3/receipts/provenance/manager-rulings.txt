[A10] **Ruling on a conflict between the specifications** (raised by R849-1 F1, PR #932 issuecomment-6930031527): Talker Advertise and Talker Failed both registered for one stream on one port.

- **IEEE 802.1Q-2018 35.2.6** replaces a registered Talker of the other type only on a received JoinIn or JoinMt. Its NOTE, which is informative, says that if both are received, Talker Failed takes precedence.
- **Milan v1.2 5.3.8.5** is normative: "If a Talker Advertise attribute with matching SRP parameters is being received, the PAAD-AE shall be declaring an MSRP Listener Ready attribute".
- **IEEE 802.1Q-2018 35.1.2.1** has a talker declare one of the two per stream and move directly between them. So a coexisting pair is a transition caught mid-way, and the newer registration is the talker's current state.

**Decision:** the split follows the normative Milan clause. The kind registered most recently decides the Listener declaration and what ACMP sees, as #665 finding 18 found the release processor's in-place swap does. The informative 802.1Q NOTE is a documented departure, recorded here and in `sw/firmware/ctrl/srp/README.md` with these clauses. Specifications are authoritative (owner, 2026-10-18), and the normative text wins over an informative note.

**When the latest kind leaves while the other is still registered,** the split falls back to the older kind. The release processor instead unregisters the sink on any leave. The README's comparison table records this difference. PR #932 keeps the fallback, because it follows the registrations that remain (R850-1 F2 keeps the order for their lifetime).
