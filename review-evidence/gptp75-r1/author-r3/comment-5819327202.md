- **Authority:**
  - Issue #75 acceptance 3 ("each defined edge case"). asCapable loss is one of the five named edges.
  - The PR's own contract: `docs/INTEGRATION.md:301` ("Tracking inside the band without an active correction | Stay inactive") and `:325` (asCapable loss).
  - The round-2 HDL guide `docs/HDL_DEVELOPER.md:125-135`: "Bit 2 means asCapable … While inactive, these writes must leave qualification unarmed."
  - The same finding class and severity as R304-1 F1, which the manager adopted as required.
- **Evidence:**
  - Reviewer mutant **X01** re-arms qualification on an asCapable-cleared flags write even while inactive:
    - it rewrites the hook to `if ((phc_slew_active_o && !st_wdata_w[3]) || !st_wdata_w[2])`;
    - the sync-ok half of the guard is untouched, so the new idle-timeout and GM-change checks cannot see it;
    - it **survives the shipped suite at 1,586/1,586** (`receipts/mutants_r2/reviewer_00.json`).
  - **The mutant is not equivalent.** Probe C (`scripts/make_gap_tree_r2.py`, `receipts/gapprobe_c_harness.diff`) runs this sequence from the inactive, ordinary-tracking state:
    - it drops asCapable with bad Pdelay responses on the wire and holds it lost;
    - it recovers capability, then consumes in-band pairs.
  - Probe C passes on the unmodified head (1,604/1,604). X01 fails its named check `FAIL probeC: in-band pair after asCapable recovery stays inactive got …1 exp …0` (`receipts/gapprobe_c/reviewer_00.json`).
  - Why the mutant is observable: while inactive, nothing clears `phc_slew_left_r` before the next consumed pair. The link-up threshold does not step an in-band first pair. The slew leg therefore reads 2, decrements, publishes 1, and raises the level.
- **Impact:** a regression in the hook's asCapable term (for example, making any capability lapse restart qualification unconditionally) would pass the gate. Entering the Milan multiple-responder cease also clears asCapable (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:105-111`), so it goes through the same term. After a Pdelay glitch while tracking, the first in-band pairs after recovery would then raise the level with no correction in progress. The parent CRF servo would discard windows and hold its integrator for about one Sync interval. The shipped RTL is correct; only the contract's pin is missing. Of the five issue edges, asCapable loss is the only one without an inactive-direction check:
  - reset: trivially covered;
  - missing Sync and GM change: covered by `:4743-4764`;
  - replacement step: covered by the link-up and locked step probes;
  - return to grandmaster duty: covered by `:4827-4830`.
- **Required outcome:** add an engine check that, while `phc_slew_active_o` is low:
  - asCapable is lost and then recovered through wire inputs;
  - the first consumed pairs after recovery are in-band;
  - the level stays low, with no edge.
  - Add a named mutation control equivalent to X01 to `tb/verilator/engine/mutants.py` that fails that named check.
- **Verification:** the engine suite passes on all three images, and `make -C tb/verilator/engine mutants` reports the new control caught on its named check. Reproduce with `scripts/probe_mutants_r2.py` against the head (X01 must be caught by the shipped harness) and `scripts/make_gap_tree_r2.py`.
