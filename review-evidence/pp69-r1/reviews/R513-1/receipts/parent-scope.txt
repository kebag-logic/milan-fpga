[A10] **Ticket re-assessment after the Mark II decisions** (owner 2026-10-06, #664 6009576644).

**The decisions it applies:**
- ADP, ACMP, MAAP, SRP and AECP move to the bare-metal core, which becomes the Mark II default.
- The all-fabric build stays supported, and stays the shipping default until F2 to F5 pass the bench.
- VERSION 3 comes with that flip.
- The split lands before the release.

It covers the 58 open tickets opened by the owner, in both repositories.

**Unaffected (no change).** Media path, gPTP, instruments, boot and release gates, tooling: #64, #74, #110, #117, #213, #260, #386, #387, #392, #393, #395, #396, #416, #417, #452, #488, #495, #578, #583, #584, #594, #611, #621, #625, #626, #629, #632, #633, #641, #642, #645, #647, #651, #657, #658, #667, #673, and processor #22, #42, #72, #134, #151.
