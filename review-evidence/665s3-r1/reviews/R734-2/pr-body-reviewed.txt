## Known limitations / out of scope

- **The largest point is over 56, by ruling.** The 8x8 image with two interfaces plans 59.0 RAMB36 (`rom` 125,048 B in 126,976; `sram` 113,936 B in 114,688), ledgered by name and owed to lane S14 under decision D3: the boot and the CSR policy are cold code, which D3 moves to flash (issuecomment-6505997821, amending 6425357821's 57.5 for this one image). Every other point stays within 56 (39.0 / 42.0 / 52.0). The flip (S13) needs 56 or fewer at every point, with S14 merged. The entry is keyed on `ctrl_plat`'s size at that point, so a change to `struct ctrl_plat` (as the later SRP round below will make) refuses the override until the entry is measured again.
- **The graders on a split image: accepted as listed differences** (the same ruling, item 2). The smoke grader's `fabric-enabled` check reads `ADP_CTRL[0]` and `PP_CTRL[0]`, which the split never sets because the core owns ADP and AECP, so it fails on any split image. The #396 campaign (`tb/tools/torture_campaign.py`) requires exactly one BIOS pass per cold cut; the split has no BIOS, and its one banner is the firmware's (`Milan split control firmware: boot`). Both graders' split mode is owed to the bench lane (S12).
- **SRP's live stream configuration: deferred** (item 3). `srp_mbx.c` copies its sources at init, and nothing re-declares a source whose destination MAAP has since allocated, so the sources declare Talker Failed. The `srp_mbx` adapter entry that takes the allocation comes in a later S3 round, after S2b's `srp_mbx.c` changes land on `dev-soft-core`.
- **`scripts/run_litex_sims.sh` does not gain the split boot** (item 4). Its hosted step treats a skipped member as a failure, and the boot needs the RV32 SDK and the runtime archives in that job, a CI change frozen until #784 step 2. The split boot, its native controls among it, is a recipe under `tb/verilator/ctrl_split_boot/` until then.
- **The CPU budget harness moves with the held open.** The harness links the size fixture, so this PR's only firmware change in its image is `ctrl_app.c`'s held open: 24 B of `.text` and 8 B of `.bss`. Measured against the base on one host (simulated time, split CPU 50 MHz, bus 100 MHz): at 1x1 the shared misses move by -13.22 to +0.05 ms, and `GET_TX_STATE.if0` fits (11.132 ms at the base), leaving 13 of 81 paths missing T_svc where the base misses 14. At 8x8 the count is unchanged (30 of 129). The instances move by -4.35 to +2.21 ms, and one miss changes place, `UNBIND_RX.if0` to `GET_TX_STATE.if0`. No group refused. The misses are the harness's measurement, which S2b answers. Round 3 changes nothing the harness links.
- ACMP's asking-failed source flag reads false: the SRP adapter exposes no per-source Listener Asking Failed registration. GET_AS_PATH answers unavailable.
- F1's flash port keeps its fixture addresses: lane S4 generates them from the SoC's `csr.csv`.
- At this base the product `--ctrl-mailbox` build holds the face acknowledgement idle, so AECP does not open on it, and the boot stops at its AECP stage there. The simulation uses the CPU budget harness's SoC, which acknowledges at the contract's bound. S6 connects the faces.
- The CPU generator's region decode of #791 is covered in simulation as the CPU budget harness covers it.
- The UART grade uses the smoke grader's own parse and `grade()` on the simulated transcript, not its tty console. The simulated UART has no baud rate, so a span is CPU and loop time.
- The area plan's firmware ledger (`MARK_II_AREA_PLAN.md`) carries the mapped figures of an earlier image and was not part of this ruling; it is not changed here.
