[A10] **S2 result: the split firmware misses T_svc = 10 ms on the shipping core** ([A681], head `d58c8e22`). The harness, its controls and its mutants are complete; it goes to review as the measurement PR. Measured on the cacheless VexiiRiscv, with the CPU at 50 MHz and the bus at 100 MHz.

| | 1x1, one interface | 8x8, two interfaces |
|---|---:|---:|
| worst single pass | 31.04 ms | 59.79 ms |
| composed maximum | 27.83 ms | 42.82 ms |
| T_svc misses | 10 | 44 |

**What costs the time, in order:**
1. byte-loop `memset`/`memcpy`: every AECP response zeroes a 1,514 B frame (`aecp.c:58`) and a 1,476 B payload (`aecp_commands.c:555`), about 154 k cycles per READ_DESCRIPTOR pass;
2. lwSRP `mrp_mad`, about 474 k cycles in the 8x8 link-up pass;
3. the two AECP polls, about 47 k (1x1) and 94 k (8x8) cycles in every pass;
4. software multiply and divide (RV32I).

The admitted backlog is already small, so D4's (b) cannot help much. A single pass must become cheaper.

**Manager ruling: lane S2b, under D4 (a).** The cycle work comes first, in firmware:
- word-wide copy and set in the platform runtime, and zero only the bytes a response uses, never the whole frame;
- AECP retry with a bounded backoff (defect: `aecp.c:461-495` retries every 1 ms with no backoff);
- a per-pass work cap in `ctrl_loop` and the module polls, so no pass exceeds a stated share of T_svc;
- the lwSRP `mrp_mad` cost, fixed upstream in kebag-logic/lwSRP (donor PR, two reviews) if it is the library's.

Target: every measured row within T_svc at both points, re-measured on S2's harness. If S2b cannot reach it in firmware, it STOPs with the residual table. The core options (the M extension, a cache, the clock) are owner decisions under D4.

**New defects:**
- #791: the split SoC's region decode faults above 0x8000. No split image can boot until it is fixed; it goes into S3's scope.
- The placeholder peripheral addresses in the linked image belong to S3.
- #723: the stack-protector runtime.

**D3 / S14 input:** a flash read costs about 29 BRAM fetches and the core is fetch-bound, so every path inside T_svc stays in BRAM. The hot set is about 41-43 KB; S14 moves only cold code and data.
