== docs/findings/B14_BENCH_5603C353.md:475-480
- **The AAF listener's FRAMES_RX counts PDUs.** Milan v1.2 Table 5.4 (FRAMES_TX) and Table 5.6 (FRAMES_RX) define observation-interval counters.
  Each advances by one at the end of every interval, at most 1 s long, in which at least one stream data PDU was transmitted or received.
  FRAMES_TX on both DUT talkers advanced by exactly 7,200 in 7,200 s, and so did FRAMES_RX on the DUT's CRF listener.
  Those three match the clause and the contracts the repository declares (`hdl/ieee1722/avtp/KL_talker_diag_ctx.sv`, `hdl/ieee1722/avtp/KL_avtp_rx_monitor_ctx.sv`, `tests/features/counters_contract_milan.feature`).
  FRAMES_RX on the DUT's AAF listener, STREAM_INPUT 0, advanced by 57,599,387 in 7,200.5 s: about 8,000 per second, one per PDU.
  Table 5.6 and the declared STREAM_INPUT contract allow at most one per interval, so this needs triage.
