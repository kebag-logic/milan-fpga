## Known limitations / out of scope

- Inputs above 0 read their current format only from LCTX words 2/3 (the CSR window). SET_STREAM_FORMAT reaches input 0 only, and no firmware writes those words. This predates the lane, but the compare now depends on it for the channel count too. The shipping 1x1 shape has one input.
- On the pruned 1x1 leg, a bare 86-octet 2-channel frame is accepted by the monitor but not emitted by the depacketizer (16 accepted, 0 emitted; the same at `4191b098`). Padded to 120 octets, 15 are emitted; padded to 230, all 16. `sim_prune.cpp` pads to 230 to keep its subject. Recommended as a new issue.
- samples_per_frame has no PDU field (IEEE 1722-2016 I.2.4.1), so no check is added.
- The flat `KL_avtp_rx_monitor` (a test instrument, not shipped) keeps the family compare on purpose. It reads only the low eight bits of the 10-bit channels_per_frame, so a channel count whose low eight bits are nonzero is accepted there, and a count of 256, 512 or 768 is rejected as if it were zero. The device listener `KL_avtp_rx_monitor_ctx` compares the full 10 bits and rejects a count other than its current format's (#765). The flat module's comment, avtp_rxmon's `[10]`, `[27]` and `[28]` text, the tsn_fuzz AAF campaign's count section and its generated record `hdl/ieee1722/avtp/doc/TEST_RESULTS.md` all say so, and none of them states the family reading as the listener's Milan rule (round 2). They describe the instrument's 8-bit read, and the campaign's section title names the counts it probes (round 3). Whether the flat instrument should read the full 10 bits is left to a new issue, if the maintainer wants one. The inherited `[27]` stimulus comment at `tb/verilator/avtp_rxmon/sim_main.cpp:453` ("any count matches now") is not one of the assigned texts and is unchanged.
- The only record of the old family compare as a 4.4.2.2 divergence is the archived `docs/history/v1/reference/PROTOCOL_TRACEABILITY.md`, which never defines current behaviour. It is left as evidence; the live matrix row says the gap is closed.
- `make dynmap-mutants` (the `[DYNMAP]` campaign) was not re-run for the `[762-DP]` change, which touches only the case that follows `[DYNMAP]` on a fresh boot.
