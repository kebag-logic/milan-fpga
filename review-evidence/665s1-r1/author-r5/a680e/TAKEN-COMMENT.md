Interpreted scope: Acknowledged state is the only truth a controller sees. LOCK_ENTITY, SET_CONFIGURATION, SET_STREAM_FORMAT, SET_STREAM_INFO, SET_CLOCK_SOURCE and SET_CONTROL change face (F) state. When such a command's commit is not acknowledged within `MBX_FACE_ACK_CYCLES`, the core records and undoes the change, keeps the final SUCCESS response, and answers IN_PROGRESS at once with the acknowledged values. Every getter, READ_DESCRIPTOR and notification then sees only acknowledged state. If every interface acknowledges within 100 ms of the command, the change is redone, and its SUCCESS and then its notifications follow. Otherwise the change stays undone, and the command answers ENTITY_MISBEHAVING with the acknowledged values and notifies no one. Either way the one final response follows the IN_PROGRESS inside its 120 ms cadence, so no second IN_PROGRESS is owed, and the command inside 240 ms. While a change waits, or any commit waits or any interface is owed, every other lock-guarded command is refused through the lock check before it changes anything. Its body then carries what its lock refusal carries: current fields, and the current `locked_id` (R701-4 F2). The automatic unlock still releases at 60 s (Milan v1.2 5.4.2.2). Its LOCK_ENTITY notification is kept until LOCK_HELD's publication is acknowledged, then sent (R702-4 F2).
