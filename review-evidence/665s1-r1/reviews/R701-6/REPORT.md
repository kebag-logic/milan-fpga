## Independent pass over the delta (before reading prior findings)

- **The lock in force.**
  - **The predicate.** `aecp_commands.c:16-20` adds `aecp_other_lock`, which is `(locked || unlocked) && lock_owner != requester`. Three paths use it: the lock check `aecp_foreign_lock` (`:25-28`), the AEM refusal-status mapping (`aecp.c:372`) and the MVU guard (`aecp.c:273`).
  - **The readers.** ENTITY_AVAILABLE (`aecp_commands.c:606-609`) and `aecp_locked` (`aecp.c:199-204`) read `locked || unlocked` with `lock_owner`.
  - **At expiry.** `asynchronous()` (`aecp.c:487-509`) clears `locked`, publishes LOCK_HELD 0 (`aecp_face.c:41` reads `locked`) and keeps `lock_owner`. Once `applied`, it clears `unlocked` and `lock_owner` together, then notifies.
  - **Readers of `locked` and `lock_owner`.** A grep found no reader that still sees "released" while `unlocked` is set. LOCK_ENTITY's `lock()` (`aecp_commands.c:469-498`) is reached only through `aecp_foreign_lock`, which is true throughout `unlocked`, because `aecp.c:347` sets `refusing` while `unlocked`. So no record of the lock can be taken or swapped meanwhile.
  - **A wait cannot overlap `unlocked`.** `asynchronous()` is not reached while `waiting` (`aecp.c:590-614`). No wait can start while `unlocked`, because every change is refused and a wait needs `notify` from a change.
  - **The ACMP owner.** It reads the lock through `aecp_locked` (`app/ctrl_app_aecp.c:10`, `app/ctrl_app_srp.c:11-14`), so it sees the same lock in force.
- **MVU.**
  - `aecp.c:271-288`: `set` requires `cmd == 1`, at least 40 bytes and a nonzero identifier.
  - Under another controller's lock in force the status stays 1 (NOT_IMPLEMENTED).
  - While `refusing` it is ENTITY_MISBEHAVING with `system_id` in force and a 16-byte body. Otherwise it is set.
  - GET (`cmd == 2`) is unchanged.
- **Egress and timing.**
  - `aecp_poll` restores `response_interface = held_interface` (`aecp.c:607`), and the held bytes carry that interface's source MAC.
  - The wait deadline is `now + AECP_WAIT_MS` (100, `aecp.h:122`), resolved at the next poll.
  - `Face.AWaitingChangeIsAnsweredWithinTheInProgressCadence` (`test_aecp.cpp:2476`) measures the final frame on the model's millisecond clock. It requires final minus IN_PROGRESS, plus the 10 ms service limit, to be at most 120 ms, and final minus command, plus the service limit, to be at most 240 ms (`aecp_latency_policy.hpp`). It never reads `AECP_WAIT_MS`.
