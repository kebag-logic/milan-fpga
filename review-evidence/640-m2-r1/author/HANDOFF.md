## 1. Inspection of the retained crossings

| Array | Built by | Stored record (depth x bits) | Base mapping (route census at dev `e8454e27`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mac_tx_cdc` | `_axis_dp_cdc`, buffered, `macdp`->`macsys` | 16 x 74: data 64, keep 8, `first`, `last` | 1 RAMB36E1 + 1 RAMB18E1 |
| `mac_rx_cdc` | `_axis_dp_cdc`, buffered, `macsys`->`macdp` | 16 x 74 | 1 RAMB36E1 + 1 RAMB18E1 |
| CSR AW | LiteX `AXILiteClockDomainCrossing`, sys->milan | 4 x 37 | 3 RAM32M + 14 FF (addr[15:2] used) |
| CSR W | same | 4 x 38 | 5 RAM32M + 2 RAM32X1D + 32 FF |
| CSR B | same, milan->sys | 4 x 4 | trimmed |
| CSR AR | same | 4 x 37 | 3 RAM32M + 14 FF |
| CSR R | same, milan->sys | 4 x 36 | 6 RAM32M + 32 FF |

Findings:

- The MAC crossings were already block RAM. LiteX stores `first` and `last`
  above the 72-bit payload. The 74-bit record therefore filled a RAMB36 (SDP x72)
  and spilled two bits into a whole RAMB18, each.
- The CSR channels were LUTRAM: 72 LUT sites in the image (AW 12, W 24, AR 12, R 24).
- The control is LiteX's: gray-code pointers, two-stage synchronizers and compares.
  It is 92 logic LUTs over the seven FIFOs out of context, about 13 per FIFO.
  No storage form changes it. Reducing it would need a different CDC primitive,
  outside this lane's scope and CONTRIBUTING's CDC rule. Unchanged.
- The resource gate allows no RAMB36 or RAMB18 growth (AREA_BUDGET, Mark II
  exception: "A primitive increase remains a gate failure").

Design: move the MAC framing flags to LUTRAM, which frees two RAMB18s. Spend
those on the two widest CSR channels, W and R. Both block-RAM counts stay as
they are. AW and AR stay LUTRAM (see section 7).

  The R channel's read data now leaves a RAMB18 in the 100 MHz sys domain; that path meets timing at signoff.

- No push, PR, merge or hardware access was made, and nothing was re-recorded.
