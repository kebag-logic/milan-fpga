Expected result / pass criteria: `git merge-base --all` prints one commit, `604899c0`, and every other command exits 0. To see the gate fail, append a line to any tracked Markdown file: `--check` exits 1 and names the first differing line. To see the cross-page rule, copy a recorded statement onto another page in underscore emphasis, with one word as a link, or with its sentence ended differently: `--check` exits 1, and `--write` adds that page's row and renders it stale. To see the named mutant, change `findings + copies(findings, texts)` to `findings` in `build()`: `--selftest` exits 1.

## Known limitations / out of scope

- Rounds 5 and 6 re-verified no statement and no citation. Statements are as verified at dev `1e80128f`, and citations as at dev `1f29f189`. Dev's merges since then are reflected only where the generator's search reads them: rows that left, rows whose statement is no longer found, and moved lines.
- The search finds the same statement, not a paraphrase. A paraphrase is caught only once its spelling is recorded under `also`.
- Inline HTML and a backslash line break are not normalised. A statement wrapped immediately before a `+` loses that `+` as a list marker, so D0-013 wrapped there is not found; no page does this today.
- Removing an entry removes its copy rows. The page says to remove an entry only once no copy row remains; no gate enforces it.
- A copy on an archive page gets a row and keeps `obsolete`. The archive stays as written.
- D0 verified the release-facing pages only. `docs/design/MARK_II_STREAM_ENGINES.md`, PR #836's lines in `docs/testing/CI_WORKFLOWS.md` and `docs/testing/TESTING.md`, and dev's other new lines were not checked statement by statement. 823 of round 2's 4,000 numeric or normative statements were read only.
- No page is rewritten here. Findings on pages held by running lanes wait for those lanes.
- The owner additions of 2026-10-30 (style-gated and diagram columns) are not applied; they await a decision.
- Two statements in the scope addition do not match the code: ETH_GUARD does not gate the LiteX PHY CRG reset, and the firmware does publish MAC_STATUS. They need a decision.
- `ci/image/tool-inventory.json` is not regenerated, and `scripts/ci_tool_inventory.py --check` exits 2 on dev's own stale declarations (Round 6; tracked in #838). The inventory step itself passes the same command-table walker.
