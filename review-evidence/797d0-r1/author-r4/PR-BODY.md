Expected result / pass criteria: every command exits 0. To see the gate fail, append a line to any tracked Markdown file: `--check` exits 1 and names the first differing line. To see the cross-page rule, copy a recorded statement onto another page in underscore emphasis, with one word as a link, or with its sentence ended differently: `--check` exits 1, and `--write` adds that page's row and renders it stale. To see the named mutant, change `findings + copies(findings, texts)` to `findings` in `build()`: `--selftest` exits 1.

## Known limitations / out of scope

- The search finds the same statement, not a paraphrase. A paraphrase is caught only once its spelling is recorded under `also`.
- Inline HTML and a backslash line break are not normalised. A statement wrapped immediately before a `+` loses that `+` as a list marker, so D0-013 wrapped there is not found; no page does this today.
- Removing an entry removes its copy rows. The page says to remove an entry only once no copy row remains; no gate enforces it.
- A copy on an archive page gets a row and keeps `obsolete`. The archive stays as written.
- D0 verified the release-facing pages only, at dev `1e80128f`. Dev's newer lines on those pages were not checked statement by statement. 823 of round 2's 4,000 numeric or normative statements were read only.
- No page is rewritten here. Findings on pages held by running lanes wait for those lanes.
- The owner additions of 2026-10-30 (style-gated and diagram columns) are not applied; they await a decision.
- Two statements in the scope addition do not match the code: ETH_GUARD does not gate the LiteX PHY CRG reset, and the firmware does publish MAC_STATUS. They need a decision.
- `ci/image/tool-inventory.json` is not regenerated. The inventory step itself passes the same command-table walker.
- Hosted result: at `808602e0` the source-list step of `verilator-lint` failed (run 41349428937) and the docs workflow was cancelled before its inventory step, so that head has no hosted docs result. Head `8d8c2e36` has no hosted run until it is pushed, so its required contexts are still to be read.
