244: ## 11. Scope and limits
245: 
246: This repository supplies the ABI, generated producer, freestanding ring,
247: segment-copy contract, workstation bundle tools, reader, and executable tests.
248: The physical acquisition link and its scheduling are board-integration choices;
249: they must preserve packet boundaries and satisfy the three-call ownership
250: protocol.
251: 
252: The ring is volatile until acquisition copies a segment. A producer can lap an
253: open segment if acquisition stalls, but the API reports that condition. The
254: workstation recovery path can return a structurally valid packet prefix from
255: incomplete input; it cannot reconstruct bytes that were never copied.
