Expected result / pass criteria: every command exits 0. To see the gate fail, append a line to any tracked Markdown file: `--check` then exits 1 and names the first differing line. To see the status rule, change a stale page's status cell in `docs/DOCUMENTATION_AUDIT.md` to `current`: `--check` exits 1 and names the page.

## Known limitations / out of scope

- D0 verified the release-facing pages only. Every other page is marked "area lane" in the Verified column.
- Not every numeric or normative sentence of the release-facing pages has its own source line: 823 of 4,000 were read only. The per-page counts are in the lane's handoff, by category.
- No page is rewritten here. Findings on pages held by running lanes wait for those lanes (`BAREMETAL_FIRMWARE.md`, `FR_NFR.md`).
- Two statements in the scope addition do not match the code: ETH_GUARD does not gate the LiteX PHY CRG reset, and the firmware does publish MAC_STATUS. They need a decision.
- `ci/image/tool-inventory.json` is not regenerated: its full check refuses at the base on an unrelated stale digest. The inventory step itself passes the same command-table walker.
- The page carries each document's line count, so a PR that edits a Markdown file reruns `python3 scripts/docs_inventory.py --write`.
