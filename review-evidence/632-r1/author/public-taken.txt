Interpreted scope: per the mechanism ruling, a phase term inside KL_mmcm_drp_servo, which stays the single media-clock master. A per-PDU phase detector compares the followed CRF stream's timestamps with the DUT's own presentation time of the matching packet-grid sample, folded into +/-Ps/2 exactly (Ps = 62500/3 ns) and averaged over the servo window. The loop becomes type 2 against that phase, with the rate loop kept as damping, a bandwidth well below it, a slew limit on the phase correction, and the integrator's dropped LSBs carried. The fixed packet-grid to CRF-output offset is removed and KL_crf_tx's event divider is phased from the aligned packet grid, so both outputs of the one clock domain fall on whole periods. Phase re-acquisition is bounded after HOLDOVER to ACQUIRE, an mr toggle and a reference timestamp step, with the restart signalling unchanged. The per-source phase contract (CRF, with AAF as the follow-on) goes into docs/design/MEDIA_CLOCK_FOLLOWING.md. No register-map change beyond new read-only status fields, no top-level port change, nothing outside the media-clock chain, its tests and its docs.
Validation plan: new self-checking simulation cases (arbitrary engagement phase, reference phase step, local-oscillator ramp of several ppm, 15 s holdover, re-engagement through INTERNAL), each pulled into +/-5 % of Ps and held, each with a planted mutant per loop term; lint_rtl --check; the full Verilator suite sweep; syn/yosys/run.sh; route-1x1 with the resource gate and the timing floor; the docs gates.
