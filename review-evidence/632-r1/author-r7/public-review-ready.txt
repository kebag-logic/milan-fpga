Changed:
- `KL_mmcm_drp_servo`: a phase term inside the one media-clock master. Per accepted CRF PDU, the domain media event less the CRF timestamp, folded exactly into +/-Ps/2 in 1/3 ns (Ps = 62,500/3 ns). The window phase is the sample sum over the nominal 256 PDUs, capped at 264 samples. It enters the integrator one window late, clamped, so the loop is type 2 against phase with the rate loop as damping (phase pole 0.944 per window, 8.9 s; rate pole 0.70), and the pull is slew-limited to 0.5 ppm. The integrator keeps 8 fractional bits (the #764 floor leak). Outside LOCKED, at an `mr` toggle, a broken reference history, or a PHC step or slew, the term lets go. `MCSRV_STAT[9]` (reserved, read-only) reads settled inside +/-5 % of Ps.
