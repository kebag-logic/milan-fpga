Why F1 is a decision, measured before any design was chosen:
- Storage. Presenting at the timestamp holds each sample for the offset less its arrival lead, about 1.9 ms at the 2 ms default, about 96 events: 128 event rows per 8-channel stream where KL_render_setpoint keeps 32. Out-of-context synthesis at the AX7101 1x1 parameters: 456 LUT (128 LUTRAM) at 32 rows, 1,006 LUT (512 LUTRAM) at 128, +550 LUT for the storage alone. route-1x1 headroom is 404 LUT and 8 slices at this head; RAMB tolerance is 0, and a block-RAM queue needs two or three RAMB18 (the parity-split rows take a write port each). The depacketizer frame FIFO is 2,048 bytes, about 8 shipping PDUs, under the 14 a 2 ms hold needs.
- Contract. Any form replaces the #386 render law (fill 8 events after accept, its rails, the #387 and #645 recentres) graded by render_setpoint, milan_dp, follow_ring, milan_dp_gm_steps and milan_dp_render.
- Interfaces. One pop per stream cannot land two interfaces whose post-grid delays differ by a frame on one presentation time without a per-interface frame delay; the Arty DAC path (KL_i2s_playback) keeps its own fill law and elastic FIFO.
Options: (a) a timestamp-snapped render queue, LUTRAM (about 550 LUT of storage plus logic) or block RAM (two or three RAMB18); (b) a presentation gate at a deepened depacketizer FIFO (one RAMB36), which also delays the loopback ring and the DAC tap. Either needs an accepted route-1x1 re-record, a supported offset range (the queue depth), a rule for late and too-early PDUs, and whether the retired Arty DAC path is in scope.
