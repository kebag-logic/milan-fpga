**Open risks/questions:**
- The notify CRF steps fail at the pin, and stay that way until the pin is adopted. On an AAF output the readback now equals the stored value. So the processor gate's planted control (compare with GET_STREAM_INFO's word) had nothing left to catch there, and the pinned processor passes the AAF steps. To keep the gate graded, the old four-step table now also runs on the CRF output, the one output whose readback differs. At the pin that fails CRF steps 3 and 4 (3 checks); with the gate spliced it passes, and the control is caught at CRF step 3.
- IEEE 1722-2016 10.8 Eq. (15) and 4.3.5 hold only for AAF offsets of whole periods, the factory 2 ms among them. With any other offset, a CRF-following AAF stream sits that offset's fraction of a period off the CRF timing points (2,010,000 ns: 0.48 Ps). The design page and the compliance row now say this; the ruling takes 5.4.2.9 there. I am recording it, not re-opening it.
- Processor PR #199's harness models the integrator as rounding every Stream Output's GET_STREAM_INFO offset; that now holds for the CRF output alone. The PR's gate test stays valid (it needs one row whose readback differs from the stored value). I did not change it.
- The builder accepts a buffer_length_ns above 2,126,000 ns, but the queue's depth does not follow it. No shipped shape declares more; documented. A builder check would be new work.
- The route-1x1 record still reads RAMB18 25 (not edited, as instructed).
