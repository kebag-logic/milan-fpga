## docs/architecture/06_aecp_engine.md:1056-1083
1056: **STREAM_INPUT triggers** (`protocol_processor_top` `stri_events`): the
1057: pbsta/acmpsta compare off the listener record write bus pushes only on a
1058: committed byte change, as on bind, unbind, settle or teardown. A discovered
1059: retry (A12, then the A5 delay expiry) retains the status and pushes nothing
1060: (Milan v1.2 §5.5.3.5.30 step 2, §5.5.3.5.10, Table 5.22). A retry with the
1061: talker gone (A17) changes the status to PASSIVE/0 and pushes (§5.5.3.5.30
1062: step 1). A repeated double probe timeout that leaves the status unchanged
1063: pushes nothing.
1064: 
1065: The committed started/stopped change under a live binding also pushes
1066: (`act_strt_chg_o`: the §5.5.3.5.6 re-bind short-circuit, and a re-bind to
1067: another talker from a bound state). For a re-bind to another talker from
1068: PRB_W_RESP that changes started/stopped, with no retained status the byte
1069: stays ACTIVE/0, so `act_strt_chg_o` is the only push. With a retained status,
1070: both listener terms fire on the same record write and their OR sends one
1071: frame.
1072: 
1073: The other triggers are the SRP registration and unregistration events; SRP's committed
1074: per-sink accumulated-latency change (`evt_tk_latency_chg_o`, including a
1075: latency-only Talker refresh, with no pulse for an unchanged value); and SRP's
1076: FailureInformation change strobe. Both change strobes feed only this notification (no
1077: Listener re-declaration, no event-router or ACMP event, [10 §6.4](10_srp_engine.md)).
1078: The SRP change strobes register on the attribute latch write alongside any
1079: registration event; the per-sink OR therefore raises one event even if several
1080: fields change together. No GET_COUNTERS rate limit applies to GET_STREAM_INFO.
1081: The two listener terms register off the same record write, so a walk that
1082: moves both pushes one frame. A START/STOP_STREAMING's own change is excluded:
1083: the command sends its opcode-specific unsolicited response instead.
