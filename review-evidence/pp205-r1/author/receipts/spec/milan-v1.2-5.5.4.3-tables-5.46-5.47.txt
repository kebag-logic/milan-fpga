5.5.4.3 Treatment of a GET_TX_STATE_COMMAND message

Upon reception of a GET_TX_STATE_COMMAND message with talker_entity_id field being equal to the Entity
ID of the PAAD-AE, the PAAD-AE shall:
     1) Extract the talker’s source from the talker_unique_id field and check that it is valid. If not, send a
        GET_TX_STATE_RESPONSE message as shown in Table 5.46 and exit.
     2) Send a GET_TX_STATE_RESPONSE message as shown in Table 5.47.
