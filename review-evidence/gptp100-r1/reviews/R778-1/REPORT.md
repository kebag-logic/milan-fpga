## 2. Clause checks requested for this round

### (1) Follow_Up information TLV after this instance becomes grandmaster

- Wire layout. `prog_leg_syncfu` (`hdl/ucode/gen_gptp_ucode.py:2399-2407`) sends cumulativeScaledRateOffset as zero (a grandmaster's rateRatio is 1.0, 10.2.8.2.1 i), 11.4.4.3.6), then reads region 6 words 0, 1 (8 octets each) and 2 (2 octets): {gmTimeBaseIndicator[15:0], lastGmPhaseChange[95:48]}, {lastGmPhaseChange[47:0], scaledLastGmFreqChange[31:16]}, scaledLastGmFreqChange[15:0] (`hdl/top/KL_gptp_engine.sv:1048-1055`). That is 18 octets in the 11.4.4.3.7 to 11.4.4.3.9 order (UInteger16, ScaledNs, Integer32).
- gmTimeBaseIndicator counts the PHC word-3 write (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:958-960, 965`). The generator has exactly one `RG_PHC | 3` write (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1990`, `prog_become`), and every path to mastership (boot expiry, receipt expiry, BTCA `we_win`) passes BECGATE, which ends at once when the plane is already grandmaster, before BECOME (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1949-1953`), so the count is once per mastership. Probe R9 (any region-4 write counts) is caught.
- lastGmPhaseChange = {pub_offset_hi, pub_offset} as ScaledNs, sign-extended (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:955-957`). The offset is the one SYNCFU publishes, "local minus master" (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1049-1055`, servo docstring). So it is new grandmaster time minus previous grandmaster time, the sign 11.4.4.3.8 asks for. See S3 for what "at the time it became grandmaster" means here.
- scaledLastGmFreqChange = floor((phc_addend - word3) x TLV_K_C / 2^16) (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:945-946, 961-962, 978-981`). The addend is the negated, clamped PI trim (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1137-1139`) and word 3 is the negated integrator, the slave's rate estimate (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1988-1990`). So the difference is the frequency the new time base keeps relative to the grandmaster it leaves, positive when it runs faster, as 11.4.4.3.9 defines it. It is zero while slewing, because word 3 then becomes the addend (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:1278-1286`), and zero before any rate write since reset.
- 25x32 multiply, at the extremes. Both operands are clamped to +-ILIM (`e_sat` at `gen_gptp_ucode.py:1122, 1137`), so |delta| <= 2 x ILIM (400 ppm). A reviewer probe copies the RTL lines verbatim (`scripts/tlvmul/tlvmul.sv` is generated by `sed` from `KL_gptp_engine.sv:945-946, 961-962, 980-981`). It compares 1,106,217 operand pairs, including all corners (+-ILIM, +-ILIM-1, +-1, 0), against exact rational arithmetic at 2, 50 (the product `milan_clk_hz`), 100 and 125 MHz. Result: 0 mismatches. The sign is right in every case, the floor is right in every case, and nothing overflows. +400 ppm gives +879,611,085 and -400 ppm gives -879,611,086 at 50 MHz, against an exact 879,609,302, all inside int32. The worst error is 1.8 LSB at 50 MHz and 10.3 LSB at 2 MHz, which is TLV_K_C's rounding (`receipts/tlvmul-probe.txt`). Probe R2 (the delta zero-extended) is caught by the harness.
- The harness's own run (`receipts/engine-run/engine-run.log`): "TLV settled: indicator 56, scaledLastGmFreqChange -791675, expected -791675" and "TLV slewing: ... 0, expected 0". So a negative delta is exercised.

### (2) Announce receipt timeout from the received interval

- `ann_rto_ms` (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:505-514`) gives 3000 ms x 2^logMessageInterval, for logMessageInterval -11..19 (clamped outside). The interval is taken from bank w0[7:0] of the captured Announce (parser `KL_gptp_rx_parser.sv:476-479`, `KL_gptp_engine.sv:547-550`). The deadline counts from the Announce's last octet in whole engine milliseconds (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:516-517, 911-916, 1291-1295`).
- The harness's measurements (the reviewer's run): logMessageInterval 1 aged after 11,999,489 cycles (5,999.74 ms) and -1 after 2,999,489 cycles (1,499.74 ms). So the port ages at most 1 ms early. For the 1 s default that is 2.999 to 3.000 s, inside Milan v1.2 Table 4.2's 2.7 to 4.5 s, as the issue scoped it.
- The ordering claim was checked against `KL_gptp_timer.sv:109-122`. The sweep starts on the tick edge and slot 2 is examined at the third sweep cycle at the earliest, so its event cannot be queued before tick + 4 clocks. An Announce whose last octet precedes the tick has its event two clocks after EOF (and the parser wins same-cycle pushes). So it is queued first, and the stale-expiry drop (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:720-734`) then discards the superseded expiry.

### (3) The three stricter readings each stay inside their clause

- Aging at the instant. The expiry dispatches ahead of an Announce received in or after the deadline's millisecond (`KL_gptp_engine.sv:519-520, 720-734, 767-775`), and that Announce is still processed after AGED. Annex D leaves unordered state blocks of different machines (Figure 10-13's rcvdMsg comes from Figure 10-12), so this is an order the notation permits. With 1 s Announces the result stays inside Table 4.2. The forced path cannot wedge dispatch: every re-arm after the expiry gives a deadline after the Announce's arrival, and the control "a dispatched expiry leaves its watch armed" is caught.
- Late Pdelay responses. `_pdpost_late_response` (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:2157-2179`) runs before the ratio window, the delay and the verdict, and ENDs with S_PDGOT clear. The next beat counts the loss, so asCapable is cleared only by the existing lostResponses path (Figure 11-8, allowedLostResponses 3, the fifth consecutive loss). CMP FMT_D is an unsigned 33-bit borrow with a zero-extended 24-bit immediate (`KL_gptp_ucpu.sv:193, 201`), so `imm = PD_LATE_NS_C + 1` = 15,006,182 < 2^24 compares correctly, and a t4 before t1 compares late. The bound arithmetic checks: (15,000,000 + 2 x 800) x 1.0003 = 15,006,100.48, rounded up to 15,006,101, plus 2 x 40 = 15,006,181 ns. The requester stamps both ends on its PHC, at most 300 ppm fast of TAI (B.1.1's 100 ppm plus the 200 ppm trim clamp). So any response a responder turns round within 15 ms of real time is admitted. A step inside the exchange is excluded earlier by PDEPOCH. The "should not" clear asCapable is a recommendation that the maintainer decision declines; no "shall" is broken.
- clockAccuracy 0x22. `CQ_C = 0xF822436A` (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:545`), and `--clock-accuracy` replaces only bits 23:16 (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:628-633`), feeding both the Announce body and this plane's BMCA vector (`gen_gptp_ucode.py:1611-1612`). The default and `0xFE` images were regenerated by the reviewer and differ only in that octet (`receipts/generator-budget.txt`). 0x22 is IEEE 1588-2008 Table 6's "within 250 ns", and 8.6.2.3 b) remains reachable through the option.
