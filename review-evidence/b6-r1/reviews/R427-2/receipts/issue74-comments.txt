[A10] Bench evidence for this issue, from bench lane B6 (PR #630, #629's bench acceptance, dev `ec0cc0c1` image).

**What was measured.** With the DUT's CLOCK_DOMAIN on INTERNAL, its CRF output stream and its AAF stream differ by 10.64 ppm:
- the reference peer following the DUT's **AAF** stream measured −10.63 ppm against the DUT's TDM clock;
- the reference peer following the DUT's **CRF** stream measured −0.07 ppm against the DUT's TDM clock.

A listener that follows the DUT's CRF therefore drops one AAF frame every 1.96 s (case A2: FAIL). Case A1 (following the AAF stream) and the DUT following a CRF talker (direction B) both PASS. R426-1 confirms the diagnosis is backed by measurement (PR #630 comment 5932376922).

**Cause, as the page reads the RTL and `docs/design/TIME_SYNC.md`:** the CRF output divides the physical audio clock, while the AAF stream is timed on a free-running 48 kHz grid. These are the "phase and frequency relationship between `clk_tdm_i`, `clk_audio_i` and `media_tick_p` in INTERNAL and CRF modes" and the "independent-divider drift" items above.

**Why it matters.** A talker's CRF output must represent the same media clock as its AAF streams (Milan v1.2 7.2.3, the CRF Media Clock Output). The fix belongs here.
