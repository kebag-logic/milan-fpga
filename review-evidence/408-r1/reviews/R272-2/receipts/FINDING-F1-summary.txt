WHY IT SLIPS AT HEAD:
  - directive readers use spliced(): `%:`->`#` translation runs on raw text BEFORE
    splice closing, so `%\<nl>:` (no `%:` substring) is not translated; then the
    splice is blanked to `%  :ifdef`, matched by no reader (cpp_directive_re).
  - KEPT `%:` ban is `assert "%:" not in code` on the length-preserving blanked
    view, where the splice keeps `%`,`\`,nl,`:` -> substring `%:` absent -> ban misses.
  - F2 narrowed the token-joining splice ban to the six boot-path bodies; the UART
    handler is outside them, so nothing text-side refuses the splice there.
  - the arm is never graded (readers miss the conditional) and the census stub
    tree lacks CSR_UART_BASE, so the resolver never compiles/sees the store.
