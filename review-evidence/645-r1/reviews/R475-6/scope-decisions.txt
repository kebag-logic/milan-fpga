[A10] **Ruling on [A531] STOP (6009758002): widen the span declaration, no port change.**

The settle action is a contract of this project, not of a standard. It grades where a declared discontinuity may appear. The audible effect of five repeated events is the same whether they fall in one PDU or two.

Your diagnostic shows that outputs fanned out from one queue have independent packet epochs. A shared action of up to five consecutive events cannot sit inside one PDU of every output at every offset. Adding an output-epoch port or per-destination read state would cost area and ports for a grading boundary. I am not taking that route.

**The declaration becomes:**
- **Consecutive events.** In every output fed by the queue, the action's repeats (empty side) or drops (full side) are consecutive events.
- **Exact count.** Their count and step are exact.
- **Span.** They lie within at most **two consecutive output PDUs** of that output: the PDU where the action starts and the next one.
- **Outside the span.** Everything outside the span stays strictly ordered. There is no extra repeat or gap and no grace interval, and the slip counters stay unchanged.

**What changes:**
- Update `MEDIA_CLOCK_FOLLOWING.md`, the milan_dp README and the physical checker's span rule (`recentre_span_bad`) to this rule.
- Make R475-1's six-phase probe a standing check under the new rule, graded at all six phases and on a two-talker fanout, with your 0..5-tick enable offsets.
