## The questions this round was asked, answered

**Is "cannot interrupt a paragraph" read as "opens only after a blank line"?**
No, not since `b7114c23`. `blocks()` carries a three-valued paragraph state and
`_opens()` gates the tag on `para != PARAGRAPH`. Against CommonMark the
reading is faithful for a top-level paragraph and deviates for a paragraph
inside a container, in both directions, because containers are flat here:
F1 is the escape direction, and the withholding direction is the two shapes
the PR declares, which I measured rather than assumed (review-evidence/
probes3-declared-residues.txt: an empty item or a quoted heading followed by a
plain line, rows J1, J2, J4, and a tag indented into the item's content
column, rows J3, J5, all WITHHOLD; the plain block quote, row J6, agrees).

**Can it ever hide a heading that renders?** Yes, and only in the safe
direction: the five rows above, plus the pre-existing families in the
SUGGESTION (a setext heading, a heading inside a block quote, a heading
indented into a list item) which the walk has never listed. In all of them the
generator omits the section and a label copied from it is refused, which a
contributor sees at once; none can grant an exemption.

**The tag grammar against CommonMark's.** Correct on all 35 probes
(review-evidence/probes-vs-gfm-head.txt rows F01 to F35, each also checked
against `HTML_TAG_LINE_RE` directly). Accepted where the specification accepts:
a space before `>`, the self-closing form with and without attributes,
a closing tag with trailing spaces, double-quoted, single-quoted, unquoted and
valueless attributes, an attribute name starting with a colon, a tab between
attributes, a name carrying digits and hyphens, `<h7>` and `<br>` which are
not in the type-6 list, uppercase, and three spaces of indentation. Refused
where it refuses: a digit or hyphen as the first character of the name, an
empty unquoted value, a bare string where an attribute belongs, a backtick or
a `<` in an unquoted value, `/ >` with a space before the `>`, an attribute on
a closing tag, two tags on one line, an incomplete tag, any trailing content,
and four spaces of indentation. Type-1 names never reach the grammar because
the type-1 opener is read first (`<pre>`, `<script>`, `<textarea>` all classify
as the block that survives blank lines), while a lone `</pre>` opens a type-7
block, which is what the renderer does with it.

**Does the gate grow a second walk?** No: `scripts/check_em_dash.py` is
byte-identical to its round-2 state, imports `TOC_ENTRY_RE`,
`generated_block`, `headings` and `label` from the generator, and carries one
regular expression of its own for diff hunk headers.

**Are the rule, the provenance basis and the CI base derivation untouched?**
