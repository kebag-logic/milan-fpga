as author-r2/receipts. For the round-3 audit, bind that folder to the published
author-r3/receipts in an isolated scratch/audit-view directory, with scratch/A1,
scratch/A8 and measurements linked to this packet's corresponding directories.
Set REVIEW_PACKET to that isolated view only for the audit invocation.
The script itself remains byte-identical to its public original.

All drivers join their children in the foreground. Two campaigns use six
simulation workers each, alongside the prior driver's three workers: fifteen
maximum. The two builds overlap their independent setup; native make -j16
commands are serialized by a packet-local lock. No shared inputs are edited.
CPU generation is disabled; missing matching cached inputs are a setup failure.

This packet preserves full numerical simulation traces. Driver metadata paths
are replaced with <checkout>, <packet>, <product-environment> or <compiler-root>
where necessary; receipt-index.json records pre/post-normalization digests.
