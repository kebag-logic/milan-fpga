[A188] DECISION (material, for the reviewers)

1. **Reading of the recorded rule.** A step needs both conditions: the pair is a *first synchronization*, and |offset| exceeds one second (exactly 1 s slews). Every other pair slews. A first synchronization is the Sync/Follow_Up pair that finds the published sync verdict (`pub_flags_o` bit 3) clear, i.e. the pair that raises it. The verdict is clear after reset, after asCapable is lost (consumption stops and the 375 ms sync receipt timeout clears it; asCapable cannot return faster than that), after a grandmaster identity change, after a receipt timeout, and after this plane takes the grandmaster role. So the first pair after asCapable is first, and so is the first pair after a grandmaster identity change. That pair still steps only above one second, so an identity change alone never steps, while the #387 acceptance-3 shape (GM change with more than 1 s) steps once. Stated consequence: a synchronized servo never steps, whatever the offset (for example a same-identity grandmaster time jump while Syncs keep arriving is slewed).

2. **Slew bound.** Before this change the rate path never saw more than 20 us. Feeding it up to 1 s would overflow the signed 32x32 multiply and program a trim far outside the PHC range. The PI input therefore saturates at +-20 us (the old step threshold). Inside +-20 us the loop is bit-identical; beyond it the trim never exceeds what the old loop could already program (about 120 ppm proportional plus the 200 ppm integrator clamp, about 320 ppm). Consequence: a 900 ms first offset takes about 33 to 83 minutes to slew, depending on the master rate (about 47 minutes against a nominal master).

3. **Documentation correction.** `docs/INTEGRATION.md` calls `phc_addend_o` an unsigned addend. The microcode writes a signed Q8.24 ns-per-tick trim, and the bench PHC and the parent consumer both treat it as signed. The integration guide will say signed.

ROM impact: only the servo leg changes (52 words at 651..702, 652..703 in the two seeded regression images). All four tracked images change; the lane record lists old and new digests.
