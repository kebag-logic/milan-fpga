## What was decided (posted as DECISION on #68)

1. A pair steps only if it is a **first synchronization** (finds `pub_flags_o` bit 3, the sync verdict, clear) **and** |offset| > 1 s. Exactly 1 s slews. The verdict is clear after reset, a 375 ms receipt timeout, a grandmaster identity change, and this plane becoming grandmaster. asCapable loss stops consumption, so the timeout clears the verdict before asCapable can return. Hence the first pair after asCapable and the first pair after an identity change are "first". An identity change alone never steps. A synchronized servo never steps, whatever the offset.
2. The slew input saturates at +-20 us (the retired step threshold). The loop is bit-identical inside the band, and the trim never exceeds about 320 ppm. A 900 ms offset slews for tens of minutes.
3. `phc_addend_o` is documented as a signed Q8.24 ns-per-tick trim; it was wrongly called unsigned.
