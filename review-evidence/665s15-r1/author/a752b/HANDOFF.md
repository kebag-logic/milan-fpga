## 7. Findings, assumptions and open questions

Assumptions made in this round, each published here and in the PR:

- **The ruling's per-interface ordinals against the contract's rule.** The tracked contract holds stream n at entry n of every interface's tables (`mailbox.yaml:338-339`: "Source s and sink k are the entity's STREAM_OUTPUT s and STREAM_INPUT k"), and today's two-interface variant keeps that. The ruling's 17 on the primary needs ordinals, so the shape's layout alone uses them (`MBX_ENTRY_ORDINAL`); every other build compiles exactly as before, which the byte-identical images show. Whether the 8x8 two-interface variant should move to ordinals too is not decided here.
- **The harness's timing at 32x32.** Two of the scripts' timing rules are scaled at a point by its streams over the 8x8 shape's 18, rounded up (4 at 32x32, 1 at 1x1 and 8x8): every bounded wait's `within`, which only bounds how long a step waits for its event (`87dd3672`: a GET_TX_STATE answered 54.5 ms after its command, past its 20 ms wait), and the owed row's settle before its composed window, which the row's defined load needs to begin with an empty event ring (`bd1200d5`, section 4). Neither changes a grade, a ceiling or a defined load; the 1x1 and 8x8 scripts are byte-identical to the base's (`receipts/harness/scripts-{base,head}-H{1,8}.json`). No two consecutive passes are idle at 32x32, so each `wait quiet 3` of its scripts ends at its bound (a `TIMEOUT` step) and could not stand in for the settle.

Findings:

- **Found after the head's gates, not fixed in this round: the new byte-identity gate's number collides with an existing gate (MINOR, Docs and Tests).** The base already has a builder "gate 40": `sw/builder/test_declarations.py:2` ("Executable declaration contracts for #400 and #403 (builder gate 40)"), which prints `[gate 40] ... output factory rows` in the same `test_builder.py` run.
