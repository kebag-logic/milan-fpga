## 5. Why STOP: the blocking limit

**The saved-state record space and slot**, owned by #823 (open, re-scoped on 2026-10-31 to 32x32):

- the builder's 16 streams per direction (`endstation_builder.py:1464`) has no rule of its own; it is the record space's 16-id blocks (`KL_nvm_backend.sv:285-287`);
- 395 writable names against the NAME block's 128 ids (`KL_nvm_backend.sv:248`, `nvm_shape.h:133`, the builder at `:2740`);
- 596 records against the 256 ids of `record_id[7:0]` (`nvm_shape.h:132`);
- a 99,080 B image against one 64 KiB slot (`nvm_shape.h:134`, `:136`).

The generator, the firmware (F1) and the fabric backend each refuse at it. Lifting it changes the KLJ2/F07.8 format that every shape's saved state uses: the record-ID width or allocation, the slot size or a compact name encoding, compatibility with existing journals and the VERSION chain. #823's scope names each of those as its own decision, so this lane cannot choose one.

Two more limits need a decision before items 3 and 5 (census section 4): the contract's per-interface tables (full at 16; 33 needed under the firmware's global sink index, 17 even with per-interface ordinals because both CRF streams are on the primary; any growth is a major change), and the datapath's fixed-width stream fields (the render crossbar's 3-bit stream field, the 4-bit talker indices, the map writer's 8-bit cursors, the 5-bit SRP row index).
