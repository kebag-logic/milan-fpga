## 6. Findings, assumptions and open questions

- **The decode defect (fixed here, `95ad6f88`).** The mailbox skeleton generator bounded each ring's host decode with a cast of `base + 4 * words` to the 15-bit offset, which wraps to 0 for a ring ending at the window's top. The contract's checks allow such a ring, and no tracked ring ends there, so it was latent until the 32x32 layout put the ACMP transmit ring at `0x7000`. The same pattern bounds the publication, face and bound-talker blocks (`mailbox_skeleton.py:295`, `:345`); `_placed` can put a block at the window's top in principle, but no layout here does. Not changed; recorded for the reviewers.
- **The owed row's validation at 19:11 did not exercise the wire**: it holds the egress for its fill and its grade reads the CPU's writes. The formal 32x32 run is what found the defect.
- **The harness changes are S2's harness's**, at the shape only: the grader reads the build layout's transmit rings, and the owed row fills the layout's ACMP ring one admitted burst at a time. At 1x1 and 8x8 both reduce to the previous behaviour (the tracked layout; a 12-record ring, one burst), and their figures are below.
- **The pinned mask's alias stays in the image** the harness measures (item 1), and no harness script binds a sink past 1, so no figure here depends on it.
- **The donor change costs 96 B of RV32 text at `-Os`** in the stack's ACMP object, the flags this firmware compiles the stack with; 1,000 B at the stack's own `-O3` Release (`DONOR-HANDOFF.md`). The pin move is its own lane.
- **The census's `aem` slot citation** was `sw/litex/flash_map.py`, which holds no `aem` row at `a9d69c77` or here; it now cites `sw/litex/milan_soc.py:140` (`FLASHBOOT_LAYOUT`).
- **Environment, not code**: this round's runner home first lacked round 3's `br-milan-rv32` link to the host SDK, and the lane's behave venv's launchers named round 1's deleted scratch. Both were fixed in the scratch and the affected gates re-run on fresh clean clones; both first results are kept.
- Round 3's other findings stand: the `nxn_window_map.gen.py` failure at the base; no two consecutive idle passes at 32x32; S2b round 22's per-item costs graded by a grader this base does not carry (#859 stays open).
- **gPTP:** no gPTP behaviour is cited or changed.
