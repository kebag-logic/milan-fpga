[A10] DECISION (manager), acceptance item 1: the licence also requires the real grant

The talker licence (`crft_emit_en_w` and `lwsrp_stream_gate`) becomes ACTIVE AND the processor's real admission verdict, `sr_admitted_o`, which excludes the optimistic `opt_r` term.

Reasons:
- **Negligible cost.** The optimistic window is three admission rounds, and a round is `N_SOURCES` cycles of the admission clock. Requiring the real grant delays a stream start by at most that, which is microseconds. Milan's stream-start budgets are orders of magnitude larger.
- **Real benefit.** A refused re-declaration no longer opens the licence. That removes:
  - the controller-visible STREAM_START/STREAM_STOP pair;
  - the Table 5.4 counter reset;
  - the possible stray PDU.

  It also matches FR-SRP-03's "on failure the stream MUST NOT transmit" without the window exception that #530 had to document.
- **No duplicated state.** The processor already exports `sr_admitted_o` per source. This is a parent-side gate change only.

Implementation waits for PR #549 (#530) to merge, because it changes the same `milan_datapath.sv` gate. It then follows acceptance items 2 and 3: a simulation of the refused re-declaration shows no licence and no counter pair, with a failing arm (the mutant drops the real-grant term). Docs that describe the window's refused branch (FR_NFR, REGISTER_MAP, EGRESS_QUEUE_MAP, ieee8021q, the CHANGELOG) are updated to the new behaviour.
