=== 6941861692 Mister-M-alt 2026-11-17T07:38:37Z
[A10] **Maintainer decision on the batched-merge details** (for the owner, by the owner-delegated advisor, 2026-11-17). The owner asked for the process of #665 issuecomment-6939460730 and issuecomment-6939574513 to be recorded as standing process. These are the details CONTRIBUTING.md 2.3 adds, with the reason for each:

- **Batch size:** four to eight PRs from one area, as guidance, not a gate. Narrowing a failing batch of eight takes about three partial runs. The current ten-member flip train stands under the owner's own decision.
- **Order:** dependencies first, then the time each PR froze. Deterministic, so nobody argues about ordering.
- **Who forms a batch:** a maintainer, on a public Issue, so the batch can be rebuilt from public state (AGENTS.md section 2).
- **The composition is its own PR, merge commit only.** A squash or rebase leaves member heads outside the base, and GitHub would not mark them merged.
- **If the base moves before the merge:** merge it in and run the candidate again. This keeps step 7's rule that the gated tree is the merged tree.
- **One fix round per finding, then drop the PR.** A second round on the composition holds every good member hostage. This refines option B item 5 ("rebuild and rerun"): the rebuild and rerun happen after the drop.
- **Narrowing:** rerun only the failing units along the ordered merges, then one full candidate on the rebuilt tree. Partial runs locate the member; only the full run counts as evidence.
- **A dropped PR** returns to section 2.1 and joins a later batch. Its reviews stay valid at its head unless its own change changes.
- **Not batched:** urgent single fixes, and shared infrastructure (gate tooling, harness grading, the mailbox contract), the owner's two classes. "Settled" means clean containment and green hosted gates on the base. While a batch candidate runs, only an urgent fix may move the base, or the candidate would test a stale tree.
- **Freezing applies only to PRs a maintainer has named in a batch** (R859-1 R859-001). An unbatched PR follows section 2.1 as before, including merging the moving base, and that merge is reviewed under 2.1 as new change.
