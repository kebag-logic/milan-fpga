## Where act shares state (why only one workflow needed a change)

- act 0.2.89 (`pkg/runner/run_context.go`, `GetBindsAndMounts`) mounts one
  volume into every job container of a replay: `act-toolcache` at
  `/opt/hostedtoolcache`. The other mounts are per job: `<container>-env` at
  `/var/run/act` and the workspace volume named from workflow and job.
  `RUNNER_TEMP` is `/tmp` and `HOME` is `/root`, both in the job container's
  own writable layer (`run_context.go` sets `RUNNER_TEMP=/tmp`).
- So a step can race another job only by writing under `/opt/hostedtoolcache`.
- `actions/setup-*` occurs only in `elaborate` (`actions/setup-python@v5`). In
  the #739 replay (`30f7b0ef`) it logged "Found tool in cache Python 3.12.14
  x64": read only.
- In the #739 replay and in both failed #745 replays (`43a25fa7`, gitlock-1 and
  patchrace-2, which ran jobs of all four workflows), every log line naming
  `hostedtoolcache/Python` belongs to `elaborate-gates-0` or `-1`.
