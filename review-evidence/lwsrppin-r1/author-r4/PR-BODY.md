## Why

The in-house qualification of 2026-11-23 found that a Listener withdrawn within about a JoinTime after the board's
own LeaveAll was never sent as Lv. lwSRP #20 traced it to the local LeaveAll. At the board's own LeaveAll, lwSRP gave
each Applicant both txLA! and the sLA's rLA!. That put a fitted declaration back in VP and resent its Join. A
withdrawal then went VP -Lv!-> VO, which sends nothing. IEEE 802.1Q-2022 10.7.5.8 and 10.7.5.9 give the Applicant one
event at its own LeaveAll: txLA! when its value fits the MRPDU, txLAF! when the MRPDU is full (Table 10-3). The sLA's
rLA! reaches the Registrar (10.7.6.6, Table 10-4). lwSRP PR #21 implements that and is merged on lwSRP `main` as
`d1bb4f4e`. Its tree is identical to the reviewed head `25b5a740` (`ba42b837`).
