Base: `dev-soft-core`. Closes #991. Refs kebag-logic/lwSRP#20 (fixed by kebag-logic/lwSRP#21).

## Why

The in-house qualification of 2026-11-23 found that a Listener withdrawn within about a JoinTime after the board's
own LeaveAll was never sent as Lv. lwSRP #20 traced it to the local LeaveAll. At the board's own LeaveAll, lwSRP gave each Applicant both txLA! and the sLA's rLA!. That
put a fitted declaration back in VP and resent its Join. A withdrawal then went VP -Lv!-> VO, which sends nothing.
IEEE 802.1Q-2022 10.7.5.8 and 10.7.5.9 give the Applicant one event at its own LeaveAll: txLA! when its value fits
the MRPDU, txLAF! when the MRPDU is full (Table 10-3). The sLA's rLA! reaches the Registrar (10.7.6.6, Table 10-4).
lwSRP PR #21 implements that and is merged on lwSRP `main` as `d1bb4f4e`.
Its tree is identical to the reviewed head `25b5a740` (`ba42b837`).

## Images

Every image differs from the base by +60 B of `.text`, all of it lwSRP's `mrp_mad.o` (13,155 to 13,219 B). No
other object, no `.rodata`, `.data` or `.bss`, and no stack frame changes. RAMB36 tiles are unchanged everywhere.

The 8x8/1if ROM is no longer exactly two banks. The base already needs 40 B past them, and its 58.5-RAMB36 ledger
adds a third bank of 2,048 B. The pin puts 104 B in that bank, leaving 1,944 B.

**LUT and FF of the firmware memory.** Mapped out of context by `syn/ooc/ctrl_memory_ooc.py` at the
shipping part and directive, base against head (the head mapped twice, identically):

| Top | Base RAMB36 / RAMB18 / LUT / FF | Head |
|---|---|---|
| 1x1/1if ROM | 29 / 1 / 309 / 37 | 29 / 1 / 299 / 38 |
| 1x1/2if ROM | 30 / 1 / 179 / 36 | 30 / 1 / 171 / 36 |
| 8x8/1if ROM | 34 / 0 / 51 / 14 | 34 / 0 / 65 / 27 |
| 8x8/2if ROM | 34 / 0 / 146 / 33 | 34 / 0 / 169 / 33 |
| every SRAM top | unchanged | unchanged |

Block RAM is unchanged. The logic that maps part of each ROM moves with its contents. The 60 B of code shift every
later byte, so 1x1 loses LUTs and gains 1 FF at 1x1/1if, and 8x8 gains: +14 LUT and +13 FF at 8x8/1if, whose third
bank now holds 104 B rather than 40, and +23 LUT at 8x8/2if. Nothing in this change can avoid it: the 60 B are the
reviewed lwSRP fix.

This is read as content-driven mapping, not image growth: tiles, block RAM and SRAM are unchanged, and the
binding check is the routed image. A routed 8x8/1if build of this head against the base is a merge condition: it
must fit and meet the timing floor (#818), and its LUT/FF totals are added here. 8x8/2if and both 1x1 images are
checked at the next composed route.
