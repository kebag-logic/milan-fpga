**What round 2 answers** ([R428-1](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/pull/631#issuecomment-5935858741), [R429-1](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/pull/631#issuecomment-5935860128), items 1 to 6 of the [assignment](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/issues/629#issuecomment-5935864862)):
- **The AAF meter.** It reads `tv`, `tu` (the common-header bit, not the `tv` net the CRF receiver takes), `mr`, `sequence_num`, the timestamp and the AAF PCM header from the parser bundle. AAF has no samples-per-PDU field, so the meter derives it: `stream_data_length` = 24 x `channels_per_frame`. It accepts only Milan v1.2 6.2's 48 kHz base format (INT_32BIT, `nsr` 48 kHz, `sp` clear, 6 samples per PDU). Groups of 16 by `sequence_num` modulo 16 stay aligned across the 8-bit wrap; every other sample count is refused.
- **Timestamp quality.** The meter keeps each group's mean timestamp, not its first. The design assumption is about +/-1,426 ns per timestamp: IEEE 1722-2016 10.8 Equation (15), plus the CRF timing points' own error. No clause bounds a talker on its own clock, and the bench lane measures the reference peer's. The jump bound is re-derived to 4,096 ns: it validates that talker and still catches a one-sample step. A desk model of the rules: with group-shaped error at +/-1,042 ns the 2,048 ns bound never validates the rate. With independent error at +/-1,042 ns and the first-PDU pick, 26 % of 512 ms windows fall outside the servo's 2 ppm lock test; with the mean, none do.
- **`mr`.** A change of the followed input, or entry into AAF following, starts a new meter era. The lock clears with no disruption trigger, and the received `mr` is re-seeded silently, so a switch raises exactly one request. The switch test drives opposite `mr` levels against a no-re-seed mutant.
- **Tests.** Every named gap gets a row with a failing mutant: the root W2 presentation, the D5 counters, the CSR words, the true-ratio leg with a mux-stuck mutant, the bound, the formats and the wrap. The decimation-by-1 symptom and the AECP-walk mutant are corrected.
- **Clauses.** (b) now separates what the clauses require, what they permit and what this design chooses. "No fallback" is this design's choice: Milan v1.2 5.4.2.15 forbids a non-ATDECC change only while a controller holds the lock, and 5.3.11.1 permits an entity-initiated change. The two 1722-2016 quotations moved to the received-toggle case.
- **Rulings.** The page now shows each ruling, #632 and protocol-processor #141. Every suggestion is taken; O1 is recorded for the model lane.
