**What round 3 answers** ([R428-2](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/pull/631#issuecomment-5936781329), [R429-2](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/pull/631#issuecomment-5936790644), items 1 to 5 of the [assignment](https://github.com/kebag-logic/milan-fpga/issues/629#issuecomment-5936791368)):
- **1. The error shape: a design fix.** No rate estimator over 512 ms can pass the servo's 2 ppm lock test against every error shape of IEEE 1722-2016 10.8 size. 10.8 Equation (15) bounds the size of the error, not its shape, and a ramp of 2J over a span T is a rate of 2J / T: 4.07 ppm at +/-1,042 ns over 512 ms. The two-point difference is the best linear estimator against a bounded error; a least-squares slope is worse (3J / T). The servo's PI (`KL_mmcm_drp_servo.sv:228-229`) multiplies estimator error by up to 2.125 in closed loop. A two-point difference over **4.096 s (E8)**, from an 8-entry snapshot ring, bounds the servo's window error at 753 ns at +/-1,426 ns for every shape (890 ns at a plant gain of 1.2). In a desk model of the page's full rules with the servo's integer PI, E8 holds 1.000 of windows with zero LOCKED drops under every shape tried: independent; alternating, random-sign and uniform per group; 10 ms, 1 s and 2 s periodic; and the worst case for the estimator (773 ns). Round 2's 512 ms rule (E1) fails several of those: random sign 0.29; 10 ms periodic 45 drops at +/-1,042 ns; 2 s periodic never locks; worst case 5,736 ns. A one- or half-sample step still restarts the history at all 16 group positions.
- **2. The void rule.** The model now applies it. The +/-2,500 ns row reads "never valid" (about 120 groups voided per second); round 2's 0.996 came from a model without the rule. The page states the tolerance the rules give: +/-1,748 ns per timestamp at 300 ppm (+/-2,048 ns at 0 ppm), 322 ns above the design point.
- **3. The switch test.** The meter owns the classification: only its own 100 ms timeout pulses `disrupt_p`; a listener change, entry or exit clears the lock silently; its enable is the one selection gate. Each mutant now fails deterministically, at the meter's ports (exact pulse counts), at a `public_flat_rd` tap on the restart request (a harness probe on a net, no port or register), or under a pinned stimulus where the new talker starts 5 ms after the switch. Checked against the unmodified `KL_media_clock_restart.sv` in the pinned HDL simulator 5.050:
  - the switch alone: one toggle per output at every phase (1,600 of 1,600 at cycle resolution; 32 of 32 at 100 MHz);
  - no re-seed under the pinned stimulus: a second toggle on both outputs at every phase;
  - the same mutant with the new talker already streaming: 17 to 27 % of phases only;
  - a request 1 to 4 cycles after the switch: on the wire in 0 of 13,056 trials, so the era-start rule is graded at the ports and the tap, not on the wire.
- **5. Suggestions:** all taken. They include IEEE 1722-2016 10.6's "due to network packet loss", D2's ruled wording quoted with both changes to it, and the merge window per output (125 us AAF, 2 ms CRF).
